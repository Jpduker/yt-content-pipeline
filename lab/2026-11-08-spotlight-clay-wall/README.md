# The Wall of I'm Fine · spotlight-clay proof (30 s, wordless, one continuous shot)
Original metaphor: every "I'm fine" becomes a brick; the wall closes him in; one true thing brings it down.
Tests: three.js clay stickmen (rig.js), heavy physical motion (anticipation, weight, overshoot), one continuous camera move,
light that tells the story (narrows inside the wall, widens warm at the end), typewriter text (caption style #4),
a real-instrument score (piano + strings) leading a wordless film, synthesized foley.
Build (WSL): `. pipeline/env.sh && sh lab/2026-11-08-spotlight-clay-wall/build.sh`. Cloud render: 720 frames in 864 s on 2 CPU workers (software WebGL).
