## 1. The market right now
- **Crowded and young.** NexLev lists 30+ faceless "what if" animation channels started Sep–Oct 2026, most in the Ifverse/GrowKaz look
  (If Then Tales, OTHERWISE, What If Lab, One Small If, What would happen?, Iflord, IFinity, Iffinity Tales, Unique Ability, DOOMPERK,
  Alternate You, Timebound, Ifinite…). Average lengths 6–10 min. Est. RPM is low: NexLev shows ≈ $1.4–2.6 total RPM for most.
- **Ifverse itself is cooling:** [Ifverse](https://www.youtube.com/@IfverseTales) 61.4K subs, 96.5% of its views from long-form. Its first wave
  hit hard ([Reaction Time 10x](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MroF1XmHOZw) 4.7M, [500 IQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4kHPfx6Gy00) 1.4M,
  [One Second Felt Like Ten](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9iB2XSmhxKo) 693K), but uploads from the last 2 weeks sit at 10–35K. It is now
  testing serialized first-person titles ("Everyone Has a Power Level. Mine Is 1." 131K, "I Have 100% Luck... But It's Not Mine." 17K).
- Lesson: the look alone no longer wins; the IDEA + emotional payoff + polish does. That is where we compete.

## 3. Winning patterns (what the outliers share)
1. **A personal power with an emotional cost** ("What if YOU could see/hear/know X"). The two biggest small-channel hits are not physics, they are
   feelings: seeing everyone's time left (1.83M), seeing how many times you'll see someone again. Twist + heart ending = shares.
2. **The unstoppable escalation** ("…and couldn't stop", "1 superpower every day", "Day 1: 6 MPH. Day 28: faster than light", "grow 1 m every day").
   The title promises a ladder, so every minute has a new rung = retention.
3. **The multiplier** (10x reaction, 500 IQ, 100x punches): one stat pushed to absurd, then shown in everyday scenes (school, sport, traffic).
4. **You vs everyone** (everyone has 500 IQ except you; everyone has a power level, mine is 1; everyone disappeared except you).
5. **Time bending** (one second feels like ten; time loop; time stopped for 5 s = the biggest what-if Short, 13.9M).
6. **Cosmic dominoes** (the sun moves closer, light slows down): physics chain reaction, Earth-scale visuals.
7. **Unthinkable numbers** (live a googol / Graham's number / TREE(3) years): cheap to make, loyal nerd audience, lower ceiling.
Structure they share: cold-open on the most extreme moment → "Day 1" normal life → 3–5 escalating scenes, each with a number on screen →
a dark turn (the cost) → an emotional or ironic ending → question CTA ("What would YOU do?").

## 5. First video: long-form or Short?
**Recommendation: a long-form of about 4 minutes (16:9), with 2–3 vertical Shorts cut from it the same night.**
- The Ifverse/GrowKaz look is a long-form format: Ifverse gets 96.5% of its views from long-form; every what-if outlier above except two is a
  6–13 min long-form; a 3:02 one (Timebound, 169K) shows a shorter cut can still break out.
- 4 min is the honest size for one night with our quality bar; aim for 6–8 min from video 3 on (more watch time; mid-roll ads need 8+ min).
- Shorts in this niche can explode (13.9M) but pay almost nothing and the winners are full 3D. Use them as trailers that point to the long video.
- GeoGlobeTales is the opposite case (98% of 1.64B views from Shorts) because its format (map facts, 60–100 s) is native to Shorts. Not ours.
