## 3. What this means for us
1. **Channels under 1,000 subs still break out** (Any Minute Now 234 subs → 142K; Ifable 598 → 128K; Lookif 890 → 133K). Each was first with a NEW hidden fact.
2. **Copies of a known title get almost nothing.** Every title we searched had 3–10 copies from the last two weeks with under 500 views
   ("See Who Misses You": one 216K original + five copies with 1–58 views; "Trust score": six videos, 2–176 views; "How lonely everyone is": five, 1–75).
   The niche is flooded with AI-written ideas, so an obvious idea is already taken within days.
3. **Length:** the winners run 7–16 min (most 10–15). Our 4:22 is short for this niche. I can't see their retention, so this is a correlation, not proof.
4. Our lane (relationships, one impossible rule, a hard choice) is exactly where the demand is. The edge has to be a fresh fact + a twist the title promises.
