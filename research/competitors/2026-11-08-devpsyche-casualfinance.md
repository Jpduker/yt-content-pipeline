**Formula (title):** "Your Life at Every Level of <obsession> (<cheap start> to <absurd end>)" — 8 of the top 10. Second formula: "Why Some People Are Obsessed With X?" / "The People Who …".
**Formula (script, from the 405K transcript):**
1. Cold open in second person on the absurd END state, concrete and specific: "At some point, you drilled a hole through the exterior wall of your house… for a network cable."
2. The thesis: "Nobody sits down and decides to… It arrives one reasonable purchase at a time." → promise: "Between the laptop and that hole there are N steps. Every one was defensible."
3. "Level one … Level seven", each level = one purchase, one real benefit (said sincerely), one comic cost, one escalation trigger ("What else could it be doing?").
4. Joke engine = hyper-specific true details (SD card dies at 2 a.m., guests get a QR code, "payroll server now runs Minecraft for a child who stopped in March").
5. Emotional turn near the end ("Level seven, the quiet part") + reframe that flatters the viewer ("You didn't build redundancy, you became it. … That's the job.").
Voice: calm, dry, deadpan narrator; music bed under everything ([music] tags every ~10 s).

## What we copy (patterns) and what we don't
- COPY: title templates, cold-open-on-the-end-state, numbered escalation ("levels"/"days"/"floors"), sincere-benefit + comic-cost beats,
  hyper-specific details, the emotional reframe at the end, 10–17 min long-form, steady posting days.
- DON'T copy (warning, per our rule): their scripts word for word, their artwork, music or voice. YouTube's "reused content" policy can
  block monetization for channels that re-upload or lightly change others' work, and scripts/art are copyrighted. Same format + our own
  words/visuals is normal practice.
- Fits us: "Your Life at Every Level of …" maps directly onto Stickman (Floor Minus = levels/floors) and could be a new stickman series
  ("Your Life at Every Level of Being Online / of Coffee / of Gym"); Casual Finance's "one number + paradox" opener fits Island riddles.
