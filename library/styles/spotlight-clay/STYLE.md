# spotlight-clay (our style, draft v0, 2026-11-05)
Learned from studying @neuronvisuals (references/README.md); built from scratch, nothing copied.

## Look
- Stage: near-black (#0b0b0e) with one volumetric spotlight cone from above (soft edges, dust motes), glossy floor with blurred reflections.
- Figures: clay stickmen. Sphere head, capsule torso/limbs, matte grey (#9a9a9e → #5c5c62 shading), tiny ink eyes and brows only.
  The hero is white and slightly glowing. Each series keeps ONE accent colour (default red #e0302a) for the story object.
- Render: three.js (capsule + sphere meshes, one spot light + rim light, bloom, reflective floor). Fallback: 2D canvas with gradient-shaded limbs.

## Motion (Jp: "heavier movements with continuation")
- One continuous shot per Short; the camera drifts but never jump-cuts. Time skips = fade to dark + re-light the same set.
- Every action has anticipation and follow-through: crouch → jump → overshoot → settle; props have weight (drag, swing, taut).
- Background extras loop small idles so the world never freezes.

## Text and captions (style #4: "typewriter title")
- Wordless or near-wordless: 1–2 uppercase lines, letter-spaced mono/pixel font, top third, typed on letter by letter, line 2 arrives on the turn.
- Dialogue: bubbles whose SHAPE carries the tone (spiky red = shouting, rounded white = calm).

## Audio
- Music carries wordless pieces (real-instrument score: piano/strings via lemo sampler); foley for every physical action (chain, wings, steps).

## Formats it suits
- 12–20 s metaphor Shorts ("…so he took the rock with him" type, our own metaphors).
- Split-screen A/B comparisons with a counter (e.g. two ways to react, across ages / days).
- Candidate for the stickman channel's emotional/second-person pieces next to the bright "what if" comedy look.
