# Retro: run after every video and after any Jp feedback
Goal: each video makes the studio better. Keep it short.

1. **Collect facts.** Time spent per step, render seconds per frame, what broke, Jp's exact words. After upload: views, retention, CTR at 48 h and 7 days (NexLev `get_my_*` tools or YouTube Studio export).
2. **Episode retro.** Write the episode folder's RETRO.md: what worked, what didn't, surprises, next time.
3. **Log.** Append dated entries with evidence to memory/LEARNINGS.md.
4. **Taste.** Update memory/TASTE.md from Jp's reaction (quote + episode).
5. **Promote.** A learning that held, or an explicit Jp instruction, becomes a one-line rule in memory/RULES.md. Remove rules that proved wrong.
6. **Improve the machine.** A step done by hand twice becomes a script in pipeline/. A wrong or missing workflow step gets fixed in workflows/. A repeated build pattern becomes a skill in skills/ (then `sh pipeline/sync-skills.sh`). Log each change in memory/DECISIONS.md.
7. **Library.** Save reusable characters, voices, hooks, styles, sounds into library/ with notes.
8. **Status.** Rewrite memory/STATUS.md (done, next up, waiting on Jp).
9. **Report.** Tell Jp in three lines what changed in the studio.
