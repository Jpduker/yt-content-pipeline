## What we take for a "whispers" channel path (and what we don't)
- Take: music-led, text-only storytelling; one emotional arc per reel (hope → ache → resolve); text timed to musical phrases;
  quiet, intimate pictures with tiny continuous motion; a comment-driven CTA.
- Don't take: their poems, their art, their music. We write our own confessions, draw our own characters, compose our own scores.
