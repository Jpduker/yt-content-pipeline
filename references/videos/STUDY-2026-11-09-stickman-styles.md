## 1. Afterhour Theory · "What If Elon Musk Took the AI Race to Space?" (znWzWYHoLOg) · 2:50 · 9:16 · 1.57M in 3 days, channel has 4 videos
- **Render:** real 3D (toon/low-poly, soft shadows, slight bloom), not 2D. Sets = small dioramas: navy sci-fi "war room" with pink windows + a
  holographic globe on a ring platform (the HUB set it keeps returning to), a running track with go-karts, data-centre aisles, factory, office cubes,
  cityscape top-down, moon surface, Earth from orbit, the sun with a swarm of panels, a white lab.
- **Character:** chibi round-head: big WHITE sphere head (≈⅓ of height), dash/oval eyes + small mouth (grin with teeth, smug, side-eye, shocked),
  hair as a coloured cap mesh, dark suit body, thin dark legs, small shoes. Identity = hair + outfit + a chest badge, not a face likeness.
  Extras: same rig in plain colours (crowds, workers in hard hats).
- **Camera:** NEVER still. Opening = fast dolly through a glass wall into the hero (0–1 s), then slow push. Typical shot 1.5–3 s; moves:
  push-in, orbit around the globe, crane down from the sky, top-down drone over the city, fly-by along the rocket, whip/motion-blur cuts.
  Cuts land on the voice (every new noun = new shot). ~60 shots in 170 s (avg ≈ 2.8 s).
- **Captions:** ONE word at a time, small bold white with dark outline, centred at ~70% height. No big kinetic text.
- **Graphics:** in-world labels (a "$1,000,000,000,000" sign over a money pyramid, a "FEED ME DATA!" tank, "TOP SECRET" folder, "THE MASTER PLAN" board).
- **Story:** second person ("You're X… you're losing… but they don't know your secret master plan"), a CHAIN: need → build/buy → next need,
  escalating scale (Earth → Moon → Sun → brain → Mars), callback to the hub globe, punchline ending ("because you already own the Earth, like literally").
- **Risk flags for us:** real person + real brand logos throughout (xAI, Nvidia, TSMC, Tesla, SpaceX). We use original characters and invented brands.

## What all four share (our rules for the stickman channel)
1. A recurring hero whose identity is costume + hair + one colour, consistent all video; extras use the same rig in plain colours.
2. A new shot on every new noun/idea (≈2–3 s), camera always moving; no static holds.
3. Second-person or "what if" narrative with escalation and a punchline/cliffhanger ending.
4. Minimal captions (1 word or short chunk), in-world signs for numbers.
5. One "hub" set that the story keeps returning to (war room / café / palace).
Our line: original characters only (no real people's likeness, no real logos); archetypes are fine (the oil tycoon, the general, the tech CEO).
