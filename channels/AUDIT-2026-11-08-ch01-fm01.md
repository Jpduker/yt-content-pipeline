## Root causes (process, not one-offs)
1. No safe-area rule: text/UI placed at the frame edge. → Every UI element inside a 5% inset; checked automatically.
2. No collision check between UI layers (HUD, signs, bubbles, cards) or between characters and props. → a per-frame bounding-box
   overlap test like the clay `CHECK(t)`: list overlaps, render must report 0 before final (except deliberate ones, whitelisted).
3. No grounding rule: actors/props without a floor contact + contact shadow. → every actor gets a floor line + soft contact shadow.
4. Long dark/empty stretches written in the script with no picture plan. → max 3 s of low-information frame; darkness scenes get
   a light source (torch, lightning) and readable silhouettes.
5. Riddle cards placed over the clue. → the card goes on the side of frame opposite the clue; clue stays unobstructed while the timer runs.
6. Stills checked at only a few times; mistakes between them are missed. → contact sheet every 2 s + the overlap report for every build.
