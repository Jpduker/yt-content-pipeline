# Confession #1 · "Now you know" (original draft, 2026-11-08)
Arc: secret → ache → warmth → vow; the last line answers the first so the loop closes. ~16 phrases on a 66 BPM score (≈38 s).

You don't know this yet,
but I rehearse your name
in the quiet before sleep.
I've practised not missing you.
I'm terrible at it.
Some nights I'm brave enough to hope.
Most nights I just hold on.
Then you laugh,
and my whole week
rearranges itself around it.
If loving you is a mistake,
it's the only one
I'd make on purpose.
So here it is, quietly:
I stayed. I'm still staying.
Now you know.
