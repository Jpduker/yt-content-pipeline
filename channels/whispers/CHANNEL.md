# Whispers (channel path under study, 2026-11-08)
Poetic confessions: music-led, text on screen, one intimate picture that breathes. Jp: "there should be an emotion that holds viewers to the end".
Studied: references/zarwhisper, references/poetic-confessions (notes in references/README.md).

## Our version (draft)
- **Look: "one-line + wash".** Each reel is ONE continuous ink line on warm paper that draws our two characters, then a soft
  watercolour wash blooms into it. The line keeps moving the whole time (breathing, hair, a hand), so the picture is never frozen,
  and the drawing itself is the motion: no zooms. lemo styles to build from: one-line (single-line drawing) + watercolor (washes).
- **Characters (original):** Ira (short wavy hair, a small hoop earring, always slightly turned away) and Sol (messy hair, tired kind
  eyes, a thin scar through one eyebrow). Never named on screen; the poems are "I" and "you".
- **Text = caption style #5 "ink whisper":** a hand-set serif, small, lower-third, typed word by word on the musical phrase,
  previous line fading like wet ink drying.
- **Music:** original slow ballads on real instruments (piano, nylon guitar, soft strings), ~60–70 BPM; the music never drops out.
- **Poems:** our own confessions, 12–18 short lines, one emotional turn every ~5 s (hope → ache → resolve), last line answers the first.
- **CTA:** a question that invites a confession in the comments (no "comment BOOK" until we have something real to send).
