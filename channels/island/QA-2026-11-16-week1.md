## Flaws found (by severity)
1. **The yellow coat is invisible when the narration names it** — S04 "A yellow coat… standing where no boat could land", S11 "On the north rocks stood the yellow coat", CH01 same shots: the wreck hull sits between camera and the Stranger. The key image of the season never appears in week 1. → blocking rule: on the rocks, characters stand in front of the wreck relative to the camera; check every "named" thing in QA stills.
2. **Clues described but not shown** — S03 webbed footprints (decals too small/dark), S05 "a small light swinging along the cliff path" (no second lantern on screen), S06 the three cables (lamp glare hides the tower top), S10 "the tunnel… wet sand… two sets of prints" (camera still outside the hatch). → bigger/brighter print decals (done), lamp glare scaled by camera distance (done), never narrate a place we can't show (the cave set exists now).
3. **Blown-out windows at dusk/dawn** — S08, S12, S14 (and CH01): a window next to the camera fills a third of the frame; S14 "dawn" reads as night. → window light now fades when the camera is close, dusk/dawn presets brighter with weaker practicals (done).
4. **Near-black night dives** — CH01 "Solve it #2", S10 last shot: only a lantern dot. → stronger night fill + hemisphere (done); rule: night dive needs `lit` ≥ .6 or a practical.
5. **Repetition** — S07, S09, S10 (and three CH01 bridges) are the same hatch framing at night; the eye gets nothing new for ~2 minutes. → rule: max 2 Shorts in a row at the same place/framing.
6. **Pip's top shots** — Pip is a few pixels at 30 m; the "eye in the sky" reads as an empty map. → top shots at 13–15 m (week 2–3 do this).
7. Minor: wreck sail is a flat card that turns with the camera; lantern glow sometimes washes Gus's face in dives; mouse/cat read small in 9:16 wides.

## Story / pacing
- Strong: every Short opens on its premise and ends on a question; the 3:12 motif and the chapter card work; day episodes (S02, S11) give the week air; the S12 "the night Gus was born" turn is the best cliffhanger.
- Weak: S07–S10 is four nights at the same door (pace dips mid-week); S05's "second lantern" is a cliffhanger the picture doesn't deliver; dialogue is rare (Dunn S02, Wren S08) — more short dialogue boxes would vary the voice.
- CH01: bridges help ("the island's one rule", the five-clue count), but two bridges are dark night shots back to back.
