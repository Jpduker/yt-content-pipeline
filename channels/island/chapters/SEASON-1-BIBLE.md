# The Keeper's Island · Season 1 bible: "The Lamp That Lies" (draft for Jp)
**Logline:** Every night at 3:12 the island's lighthouse lamp goes dark for exactly twelve seconds. Ships are starting to wreck.
Gus, the keeper, swears it isn't him. With his crew he solves one riddle after another to find who is signalling to the sea, and why.

**Cast (consistent every chapter):** Gus (keeper, warm, stubborn, voice am_michael) · Pip (seagull, show-off, sees everything from above) ·
Mabel (cat, sleepy, sharpest mind) · Rollo (mouse, hungry, guilty-looking, secretly brave) · **new:** the Stranger in the yellow coat
(seen only from behind; never speaks; the season's mystery) · Old Wren (the island's postmistress; knows the Old Tales).
**Rules:** every chapter = 3 riddles the viewer can solve (pause-and-solve countdowns), each answer opens the next door; every chapter ends on a
cliffhanger; every Old Tale (Thursday Short) hides one clue. The answer to the season is fair: all clues are on screen before the reveal.

## Chapter 1 · "Twelve Seconds of Dark" (≈5 min, 16:9 long-form) · beat sheet
0:00 Cold open. 3:11 a.m., storm. The lamp turns… and dies. A ship's horn in the dark. Twelve seconds. The lamp returns by itself. Title.
0:30 Morning. A boat wrecked on the north rocks; nobody hurt. The harbour master blames Gus. Gus has 3 days to prove it wasn't him.
1:00 **Riddle 1 (the clock):** the lamp room clock is 12 seconds slow, but no one touched it: its pendulum was moved — by whom? (clue: wet
     webbed footprints only Pip could leave… no: they're too big for a gull). Countdown. Answer: a frog? No — the footprints are a diver's fins.
     Someone came up FROM the sea. Gasp beat.
2:00 The crew stakes out the night. Mabel naps (of course). Rollo eats the stake-out sandwiches. Comedy beat, then 3:12: dark again.
2:40 **Riddle 2 (the cable):** three cables, one cut, two whole; the cut one is dry, the others wet. Which way did the cutter leave? Answer: by
     the stairs, not the window — and the stairs were painted this morning (callback to EP02): yellow prints lead to the cellar.
3:40 **Riddle 3 (the cellar door):** a lock with four symbols from an Old Tale (OT01's shadow mark). Viewers who saw the Tale can solve it.
     Answer: the order of the four prints in "The Man Who Sold His Shadow".
4:30 The door opens on a tunnel to the sea, and in the wet sand… two sets of footprints. One of them has no shadow on the wall behind it.
4:50 **Cliffhanger:** the lamp goes dark — at noon. The Stranger in the yellow coat stands on the rocks, facing the lighthouse. Cut. "Chapter 2: Saturday."
**Shorts cut from it (Mon/Wed/Fri):** Riddle 1, Riddle 2, Riddle 3 — each 30–45 s, each ending "the answer is in Chapter 1".
**Look upgrade (vs EP01/EP02):** 16:9 long-form sets with depth (3 parallax layers), real night/storm lighting passes, rain, the lamp's
beam as a moving light source, characters with walk cycles and turnarounds, camera moves that follow action (no slides).
