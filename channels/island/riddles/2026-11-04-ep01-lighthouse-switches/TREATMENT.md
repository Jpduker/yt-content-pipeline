# Episode 01: "The Lighthouse Switches" (riddle Short)

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (lemo `midcentury-toon`). Format: YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~40 s, English narration (Kokoro, offline), burned captions + .srt, −14 LUFS.
Riddle: the classic "three switches, one bulb" logic puzzle, re-staged in an original lighthouse world. (Logic puzzles are not copyrightable; future episodes should use original riddles.)

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **The climb (chosen).** Hook on three switches → the camera rides the lighthouse up to the dark lamp to show the distance → three rules stamp in as medallions → countdown → the keeper does the trick and climbs while a step counter runs → three outcomes in a split frame → the camera falls back to the switches, which is the first frame (seamless loop). Why: it uses the tall vertical frame for its real job (distance = the constraint), the camera move *is* the rule, and it loops.
2. **Wrong answers first.** The keeper climbs three times (fails the "only once" rule) in fast comic cuts, then the narrator stops him and asks for a smarter way. Funnier, but spends 10 s on failure before the question and wastes the hook.
3. **Split screen do / don't.** Left: dumb method, right: smart method, side by side. Clear, but halves a vertical frame and kills the countdown tension.

## 2. Logline and arc
A lighthouse keeper must find which of three switches lights the lamp 120 steps up, but may climb only once.
Setup: switches, the distant lamp, the rules → Turn: the countdown, then "the trick": heat → Ending: three outcomes, and we land back on the switches, ready to loop.

## 5. Cue map
120 BPM (beat 0.5 s), 4/4, 20 bars. Cool-jazz combo, synthesized: vibraphone (chords, tremolo), pizzicato walking bass, brushes, finger snaps, celesta pings.
- Bars 1–2 (hook): vibes + bass, snaps on 2 and 4. Switch "hover" on beat 3.
- Bars 3–4 (crane): walking bass climbs chromatically with the camera; vibes glissando up into the lamp.
- Bars 5–7 (rules): each medallion stamps on beat 1 of its bar (rubber stamp).
- Bars 8–10 (countdown): bass only + mantel-clock tick on every beat; each number on a downbeat.
- Bar 10.5–11 (still): everything cut, room tone only.
- Bars 11–16 (reveal): full combo returns on "Here's the trick"; switch clicks on beats; clock whirr for "5 minutes".
- Bars 17–20 (payoff/CTA): celesta melody, vibes tremolo chord; the last bar resolves into the bar-1 groove so the loop is seamless.
