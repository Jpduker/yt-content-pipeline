Work only on Vice City Alibis unless asked to touch another channel.
Read START_HERE.md, docs/DECISIONS.md and the relevant visual/audio/narrative bible before a new episode.
Preserve the approved Liam and Laura settings and existing references. Liam is preferred.
Never save API credentials, request authorization headers, or private account data in this project.
Lara is a requested candidate with API generation blocked by the current free account. Do not silently substitute Laura and label it Lara.
Differentiate canonical GTA VI names/places, original fan-fiction events, and original supporting characters.
Use existing visual references before generating a new pose, wardrobe or location; keep a continuity sheet per episode. Give every recurring character an identity record, a six-view board and a four-outfit closet before recurring use.
Review hands, photo orientation/content, wheel contact, vehicle identity, empty/occupied cars, props and time of day at full size.
Narrative structure, POV and delivery are independent choices. Choose them for the story.
Keep speech intelligible and centered; export dry voice, music, ambience, effects and final mix separately.
Record API/model/settings, prompt, source and licensing status for each generated audio asset.
Do not create a YouTube channel, reserve handles, upload or publish until the user explicitly authorizes those actions.
These free-plan ElevenLabs voice/SFX generations are review assets. Resolve commercial licensing and regenerate release audio as necessary before commercial distribution.
