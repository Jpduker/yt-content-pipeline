# Review before the next production
- Story: immediate dilemma, understandable cause/effect and earned payoff.
- Delivery: chosen narrator/dialogue/POV supports this story; cast stays understandable.
- Visuals: identity, hands, photo content/orientation, tires, vehicle occupants and props checked at full size.
- Sound: speech clear at low volume; rain changes perspective; music follows emotion; foley belongs to actions; final line has room.
- Export: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, captions in a safe area, complete decode and peak check.
- Provenance: source assets, prompts, voice IDs/settings and model/provider receipts saved.
- Release: separate review approval from authorization to create/upload/publish; current audio commercial status is documented.
