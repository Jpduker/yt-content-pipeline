# Visual bible
Approved look: premium painted crime graphic novel; recognizable Jason and Lucia fan-art identities; realistic anatomy; rich tropical neon; magenta and teal with amber skin/lamps; atmospheric depth and controlled texture.
Maintain realistic car dimensions, tire contact, object perspective, hands and cloth detail. Richness must survive phone-size display.

## Character reference hierarchy
1. Approved v2 face/scene references.
2. New turnaround boards, after checking identity against level 1.
3. Wardrobe boards, after checking the same face/body/hair.
4. Episode-specific variants generated from those references.
The new clothes are fan-fiction wardrobe proposals, not claims about in-game unlocks or exact canon costumes.

Each current pilot character, including the original fixer, has a six-view reference board, a four-outfit closet board and identity metadata. Six views are front, left three-quarter, left profile, back, right profile, right three-quarter. Generated boards are references rather than calibrated 3D renders.
Supporting characters receive their own references before reuse. The original fixer is stored separately.

## World
Preserve approved neon motel, cargo docks and waterfront references. Store weather, time, light direction, camera viewpoint and scale with each new location.
The atlas is an illustrative style reference, not an exact game map.
Officially named regions are catalogued separately from invented fan-fiction sets. New exploration candidates include Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

## Episode continuity sheet
Record scene number, location/time/weather, characters, view, outfit ID, prop state, vehicle identity/occupants, photographic print reference, entry/exit positions and source asset paths.
Check photo fronts/backs and unchanged content across shots. A paper grip must connect plausible fingers to wrists.
Wheels need consistent axles, rims and contact surfaces; vehicles on a flatbed need a sufficiently large deck and plausible restraints.
Do not place the heroes in an empty decoy vehicle or duplicate that same vehicle at their escape location.
Keep clothes and bag contents consistent. A cash discovery inside a car must use an interior image.
