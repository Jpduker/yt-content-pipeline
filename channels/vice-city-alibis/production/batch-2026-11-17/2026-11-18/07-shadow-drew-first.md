# His Shadow Drew First

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 127 spoken words.

## Hook

The Fixer's shadow raised a gun. Jason saw it before the pistol cleared the table.

## Exact narration

The Fixer's shadow raised a gun. Jason saw it before the pistol cleared the table. Lucia stood opposite him. Their stolen diamonds sat beside his payment case. He wanted both. Jason dropped his lighter. Lucia followed his glance toward the wall and understood the warning. He ducked. She shoved the steel payment case into the Fixer's rising hand. One shot. The ceiling took it. Jason grabbed the falling pistol while Lucia kicked the table forward. Pinned behind his chair, the Fixer watched Jason take the diamonds and Lucia take their payment. They backed onto the dock, keeping him in sight until the boat engine caught. No chasing ghosts. Jason checked the cash beneath the deck lamp. Lucia kept the lighter. Next time, she wanted a louder warning.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Survive the Fixer double-cross and leave with their diamonds and agreed cash.
- **Stakes:** A concealed pistol is coming up before payment is complete.
- **Obstacle:** Close-range armed betrayal across a small table.
- **Planted Clue:** Low southwest lamp projects the rising gun shadow onto the north wall before the pistol clears the table.
- **Payoff:** Jason nonverbal warning lets Lucia interrupt the draw; their coordinated movement wins both prizes and the lighter becomes a closing joke.
- **Causal Check:** A single shot hits an established empty ceiling. Gun ownership transfers once to Jason; table and chair delay the Fixer for seconds while the heroes keep him in view and reach an already-moored escape boat. No unexplained third rescuer.
