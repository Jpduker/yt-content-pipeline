# Lucia Recognized the Broken Clasp

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 134 spoken words.

## Hook

The auction necklace was fake. Lucia knew because she had bent the real clasp stealing it.

## Exact narration

The auction necklace was fake. Lucia knew because she had bent the real clasp stealing it. The Fixer smiled. Jason reached for the case. Lucia stopped him. Wrong prize. Beneath the Fixer's catalogue, one gold link caught the light. He kept the original for himself. Lucia demanded a closer look at the fake. He lifted it, leaving his catalogue unguarded. Jason pinned the catalogue with his palm. The Fixer grabbed his wrist. Every buyer turned toward the struggle. Lucia pulled the real necklace from beneath the paper and held up its bent clasp beside the perfect copy. The room erupted. Buyers blocked the Fixer, demanding their deposits back. Jason and Lucia took the terrace stairs. Outside, she pocketed their necklace. Jason asked about the damaged clasp. Lucia said it had just paid for itself.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Recover their real stolen necklace before the Fixer sells a copy and keeps the original.
- **Stakes:** Losing the original and being exposed as thieves in a room of criminal buyers.
- **Obstacle:** Fake display piece, concealed original and Fixer physical resistance.
- **Planted Clue:** Lucia bent the original clasp during its theft; a protruding link reveals the hidden original before Jason acts.
- **Payoff:** The visible mismatch proves the Fixer scam to his own buyers, whose refund demands block pursuit while the heroes keep their original.
- **Causal Check:** There are exactly two necklaces, each tracked from introduction to final owner. Buyers react to proof of fraud, not unexplained loyalty to heroes. East terrace stairs are shown before escape.
