## Exact narration

Jason opened locker sixty-nine. Empty. Footsteps hammered behind the showers. Their stolen diamonds were missing. The Fixer had handed them one key, then vanished. Lucia studied the numbers beneath a swinging light. A line sat above them. She turned the tag. Ninety-six. Jason had followed it upside down. They reached the other aisle. A cleaning cart blocked the door, and the key refused to turn. Jason pulled harder. Lucia stopped him. The cart wheel was pressing the locker door against its latch. She kicked the cart clear. The lock turned. Inside sat their blue pouch, still sealed with red thread. They slipped through the shower exit as the Fixer reached the wrong aisle, following their open locker. On the seawall, Jason counted every diamond. Lucia hung the key upright around his neck. Numbers mattered tonight.
