# The Money Went Down First

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 135 spoken words.

## Hook

Jason threw their cash overboard. Lucia grabbed the strap before it cleared the roof railing.

## Exact narration

Jason threw their cash overboard. Lucia grabbed the strap before it cleared the roof railing. Below them, the fire escape ladder hung above street level. Their roof door shook under boots. Jason wanted to jump. Lucia pointed below: the ladder's top rung hung just beyond their reach. She hooked their bag strap around its top rung. Their entire score became a hanging weight. The ladder slid downward. Then the strap caught a rusty bolt, leaving the bottom rungs above the pavement. Lucia climbed onto the fixed platform and pulled the bag sideways. Jason held her belt until it freed. The ladder settled. Jason climbed down first, unhooked the money, then steadied the rungs while Lucia followed. They left with every dollar as the roof door burst open. Jason decided gravity could have a cut: nothing.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Escape a rooftop with their stolen cash before pursuers break through.
- **Stakes:** Being cornered on the roof or losing the score in a desperate jump.
- **Obstacle:** Raised counterbalanced ladder and a snagged bag strap.
- **Planted Clue:** A low rear annexe places the heroes beside the reachable ladder head; counterweight and platform geometry are established first.
- **Payoff:** They use the cash as temporary weight, free the snag together, deploy the normal ladder stop and reclaim every dollar.
- **Causal Check:** No distant ladder is magically within reach. The fixed platform supports Lucia, Jason braces her, and the deployed stop keeps the ladder down after the bag is removed.
