# The Counting Machine Was Lying

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 134 spoken words.

## Hook

The machine swallowed ten thousand dollars. The Fixer announced Jason had delivered six.

## Exact narration

The machine swallowed ten thousand dollars. The Fixer announced Jason had delivered six. Jason reached across. Lucia stopped him. Beneath the machine, a loose bill fluttered into a hidden slot. She folded the corner of one note and fed it through. The counter stayed still. Missing. The Fixer covered the reject drawer. Lucia pointed at his hand. Jason dragged the heavy machine toward himself. Its hidden chute tore free from the desk. Missing bundles spilled behind the Fixer's shoes. He lunged for them. Jason shoved the machine sideways across the narrow aisle while Lucia scooped up their cash. They backed through the service door together, watching his hands. The machine blocked him long enough to latch it. Outside, Jason counted the money himself. Ten thousand exactly. Lucia said the machine needed a better accountant.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Collect their full delivery payment instead of accepting a rigged count.
- **Stakes:** The Fixer is stealing forty percent while claiming the heroes shorted him.
- **Obstacle:** Rigged hidden cash chute and Fixer physical obstruction.
- **Planted Clue:** A bill visibly flutters through an underside slot; Lucia marked-note test confirms the diversion.
- **Payoff:** They expose the catch tray, recover every dollar and use the heavy machine as a temporary aisle block.
- **Causal Check:** The money stays in one room and never changes total. The machine has a physical hidden chute, not magic software proof; the marked note is recovered. Escape buys seconds, not permanent imprisonment.
