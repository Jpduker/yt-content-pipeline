Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 135 spoken words.

## Exact narration

The Fixer paid them in newspaper. Jason swore. Lucia thanked him, then lifted his coat from the chair. He answered his phone. They left before he noticed his missing jacket. In its pocket, she found the valet ticket. Jason remembered the steel payment case beneath the Fixer's car seat. Downstairs, the garage kiosk swallowed the ticket and released a key pouch. His coupe unlocked. Jason opened the passenger door. The case was chained beneath the seat. Lucia handed him the coat again. Inside its other pocket sat a brass key. The Fixer had carried his entire security system upstairs. They drove out with the case. His windscreen pass raised the barrier as he reached the garage stairs. At the next light, Lucia opened their payment. Jason offered to return the coat. She checked the weather.

- **Goal:** Take the agreed real payment after the Fixer substitutes newspaper.

- **Planted Clue:** His coat holds the valet claim token and padlock key; the steel case is visibly under the passenger seat before garage entry.
- **Payoff:** They recover payment and steal his coupe and coat, exiting with his own parking pass.
- **Causal Check:** The invented private garage explicitly uses token retrieval. Ticket releases the car key, second pocket supplies the case key, windscreen pass controls barrier; these are three distinct physical items with tracked states.
