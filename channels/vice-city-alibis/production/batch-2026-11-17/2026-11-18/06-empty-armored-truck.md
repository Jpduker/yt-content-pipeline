# The Empty Truck Was the Score

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-18 story. Target: 56 seconds, 133 spoken words.

## Hook

Jason stole an armored truck. He opened the back and found nothing. Lucia started laughing.

## Exact narration

Jason stole an armored truck. He opened the back and found nothing. Lucia started laughing. The clipboard said collection, not delivery. Their empty truck was due at the Fixer's cash warehouse. Jason wanted another target. Lucia handed him the truck's collection helmet and pulled the passenger visor down. At the gate, the Fixer checked the route placard, then waved his masked collectors toward the loading cage. The first cash bag landed inside. Then he demanded their signature before releasing the second. Jason froze. Lucia pointed at the printed driver name and held out the clipboard. Jason copied it, visor still lowered. Both bags aboard, they left just as the real collection crew stepped from a taxi behind them. Jason finally understood. They hadn't stolen an empty truck. They had stolen its next stop.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Turn an apparently worthless empty truck theft into a full cash score.
- **Stakes:** Being recognized by the Fixer or reaching the depot after the real collection crew.
- **Obstacle:** No cash aboard, identity concealment and a last-minute paperwork demand.
- **Planted Clue:** The existing clipboard says collection, and matching helmets/route placard already belong to the stolen truck.
- **Payoff:** They steal the scheduled pickup itself and leave with both cash bags before the real crew arrives.
- **Causal Check:** A clearly fictional depot relies on vehicle placard, covered collection crew and a routine signed manifest. No invented hacking, perfect signature copy or disappearing guards. Entry and exit lanes are separate and the real crew arrives only after loading.
