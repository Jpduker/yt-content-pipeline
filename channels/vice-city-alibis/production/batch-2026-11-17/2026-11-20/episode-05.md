# We Stole The Dock Too

20 November 2026 · 133 words · 56 seconds estimated · Assets PLANNED

Original unofficial GTA VI fan fiction. Specific sets, events, dialogue and supporting character The Fixer are invented. No actual gameplay or story claims.

## Script

The dock followed their stolen boat into the bay. Earlier, Jason called her plan impossible. They needed the courier skiff before its owner returned. Its locker held their robbery recording. Jason found the ignition key. Lucia found the chain: hardened steel around a mooring post. He reached for bolt cutters. Their jaws chipped. Across the marina, a security door opened. Lucia stamped the decking. Rotten wood crumbled around the post's four bolts. The lock was stronger than the dock. She looped the chain over the stern roller. Jason eased the engine forward while she watched the post lean. One plank cracked. Then another. The entire little mooring platform tore loose, leaving the main pier intact. They cleared the marina towing splintered evidence. Lucia opened the locker. Jason finally stopped laughing at her plan.
