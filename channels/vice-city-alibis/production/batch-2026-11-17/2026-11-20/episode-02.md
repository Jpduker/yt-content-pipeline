## Script

I had ninety seconds to steal my own car before the crusher made my last payment permanent. Lucia said we should leave it. Only that engine could clear our debt. The impound lane was blocked by two wrecks. My coupe sat on four little wheeled skates, facing concrete. I turned the ignition. Nothing. They had removed the battery, A forklift approached. Lucia pointed at the skates. Their wheels swiveled. We only needed to push. She released their brakes while I pulled the side gate open. The gap was narrower than the car. Narrower than its length, Lucia corrected. Together we rolled it sideways through the opening, engine cold. Outside, I touched the hood. She held out her hand. Half the sale, she said. Including your sentimental attachment.
