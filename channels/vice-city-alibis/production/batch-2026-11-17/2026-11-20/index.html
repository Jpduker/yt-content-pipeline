Beside its call button, somebody had scratched a ladder. Jason loaded the safe. The platform dropped, leaving them facing a locked stairwell and approaching security lights. He grabbed a chair to smash through. Lucia caught his wrist. Broken glass would announce their floor. She followed the scratched symbol to a maintenance hatch. Behind it, a fixed service ladder descended. Jason climbed first. Lucia followed, reaching the grounded platform just as security unlocked the upstairs stairwell. She opened their safe and pocketed both passports. Next job, she said, you ride with the luggage.
