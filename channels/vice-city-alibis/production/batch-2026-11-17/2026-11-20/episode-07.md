# The Exit Was In The Reflection

20 November 2026 · 134 words · 56 seconds estimated · Assets PLANNED

Original unofficial GTA VI fan fiction. Specific sets, events, dialogue and supporting character The Fixer are invented. No actual gameplay or story claims.

## Script

Jason had the emerald. Lucia had the key. Neither could see the exit. The panic shutter divided the showroom. Jason was trapped beside the vault, Lucia beside the security desk. He wanted to break the divider. She pointed at the red alarm wire around its edge. Below sat a labeled release panel. Her side had no keyhole. She passed the key through a slot. Jason checked his side. Four keyholes. No labels. A tilted mirror reflected the switches. Lucia read the panel: third from left. In the mirror, that meant second. Jason reached wrong anyway. She tapped the glass twice. He counted from her reflection, turned the second keyhole, and the service door opened. Jason slipped through with the emerald. Lucia took his hand. Next time, she said, steal something that comes with directions.
