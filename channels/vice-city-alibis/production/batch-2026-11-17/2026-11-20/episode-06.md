# The Fixer Bought The Wrong Silence

20 November 2026 · 130 words · 56 seconds estimated · Assets PLANNED

Original unofficial GTA VI fan fiction. Specific sets, events, dialogue and supporting character The Fixer are invented. No actual gameplay or story claims.

## Script

The Fixer wanted ten thousand for silence. Lucia brought a cheap cassette player. He owned the only tape of Jason threatening a courier. Tonight he would auction it. Jason brought the cash. Lucia brought a cassette with its safety tabs snapped off. The Fixer laughed. He demanded their copy. Jason reached for it. Lucia stopped him and pointed at the player's red button. The Fixer had pressed record, not play. He wanted to erase their proof before their exchange. The missing tabs blocked recording. Jason pushed play, exposing the Fixer's auction pitch. They had recorded yesterday's negotiation. Sell Jason's threat, Lucia said, and every bidder hears you offering their names to police. The Fixer surrendered the original. Jason pocketed both tapes. Lucia left the player. He might learn its buttons.
