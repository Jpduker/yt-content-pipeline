# The Second Toast

## Script

The Fixer offered Jason a toast, then slid Lucia the only glass with a paper coaster. They'd come to trade his stolen ledger for the name of whoever ordered their execution yesterday. Jason reached for his drink. Lucia caught his wrist. The coaster carried a faint blue ring. Same ink as the ledger's page numbers. Beneath it, writing pressed through from another page. The Fixer demanded proof they had it. Jason started reaching inside his jacket. Lucia ordered another toast. This time she switched the glasses. The Fixer knocked his over before it touched his mouth. Jason kept the ledger. Lucia took the wet coaster: beneath the ring was a private dock number. They hadn't discovered the killer's name. They'd found where their host was sending the next payment.

## Story audit

- **goal:** Trade stolen ledger for identity of the person who ordered their deaths.
- **stakes:** A second attempted killing occurs at the negotiation itself.
- **obstacle:** Fixer demands ledger before answering; Jason nearly drinks while Lucia has only circumstantial warning.
- **planted clue:** Blue ring matches ledger ink, suggesting marked serving; pressure impressions beneath it show coaster used as a writing pad.
- **payoff:** Switching the glasses provokes an incriminating refusal; the spilled drink exposes an indented dock number on the coaster.
- **causal check:** The glass swap is a test, not magical poison detection; host's fear confirms danger. Wet coaster reveals preexisting pressure writing. They retain leverage and gain a destination rather than falsely solving the killer's identity.
