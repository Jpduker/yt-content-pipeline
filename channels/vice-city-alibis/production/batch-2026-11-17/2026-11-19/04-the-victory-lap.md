## Script

You're winning, Jason. That's the problem. The prize is the title to Lucia's seized red coupe. She spotted the catch: winners get searched upstairs. Losers collect their deposits beside the impound gate. Your copied booth key is inside your helmet lining. Win, and security takes your only way in. You say you can outrun them. She points at the recovery truck blocking the exit behind the finish. So you lift off. One rival passes. The crowd boos the easiest victory you've ever thrown. Downstairs, you surrender your helmet as collateral. Lucia palms the key while the clerk counts your refund. She opens the booth during the winner's search. You use your refunded impound token to release her coupe. The trophy goes upstairs. The title comes down. Losing finally gets you both something worth keeping.

## Story audit

- **goal:** Recover Lucia's seized red coupe and physical title.
- **stakes:** Winning brings a search and loss of copied key, extending the promoter's hold on her car.
- **obstacle:** Jason wants victory; truck blocks forcing an exit.
- **planted clue:** Separate search and deposit routes; refundable round token on registration receipt.
- **payoff:** Losing enables key transfer during refund, token opens gate, winner's search distracts upstairs staff.
- **causal check:** Key planted before race; token established before refund; booth and car separate but connected by stairs. Physical title retrieval removes promoter leverage, not instant legal ownership transfer.
