# The Empty Cart

## Script

Jason spent their escape money buying ten gold bars. Lucia wanted to steal the empty cart. He thought she was angry. She pointed at the floor: the loaded cart left no marks. The empty one had gouged two tracks through the shed's green paint toward the exit. Jason lifted a bar. Too light. The seller had parked the real weight inside the other cart's base. He wanted his money back. Lucia watched the shutter descend. Asking politely would cost them their exit. She shoved the fake bars beneath the shutter. Jason tipped the heavy cart until its false bottom split. Gold hit concrete. So did the receipt bearing Jason's signature. Lucia made him pick that up first. They rolled out under the jammed shutter. Jason got his fortune. Lucia got control of the next deal.
