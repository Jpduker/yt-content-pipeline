# The Brake Light

## Script

Our getaway car braked without a driver. That was when Lucia stopped calling my plan stupid. Twenty minutes earlier, we'd entered impound to steal the coupe holding her recorded confession under its dashboard. She wanted the tape. I wanted the engine. We argued until its brake lights blinked again. Nobody inside. No keys. But a thin cable ran from the pedal into the inspection office. The recorder wasn't evidence storage. It was bait, wired to trap whoever opened the driver's door. I pulled the cable through the fence instead. The office chair rolled forward, dragging its hidden camera. Back at the blinking lights, Lucia lifted the tape through the passenger window while I held tension. We left the engine. We took the camera too. Its first clip showed the Fixer installing it.

- **obstacle:** Jason wants the valuable engine while a cable trap guards the driver door.
- **planted clue:** Brake lights activate while the car is empty; cable runs pedal to office.
- **payoff:** Controlling cable exposes camera; passenger-window access recovers tape. Camera setup clip identifies the original Fixer as installer.
