## Script

Lucia stole the auction paddle because stealing the painting would get Jason killed before sunrise. The Fixer owned their debt. Tonight, his secret buyer was collecting a stolen canvas as payment. Jason wanted to slash it. Lucia noticed wet ink on the buyer's bid sheet: tomorrow's date. The auction had already happened. Tonight was a rehearsal, with their fingerprints supplying the missing thieves. Jason called her paranoid. Then she turned the canvas over: its shipping label named his apartment. Lucia took the paddle into the glass booth and raised the buyer's ceiling by two million. He panicked, reached for the emergency cancellation key, and exposed the original painting behind his chair. They left the fake, took the original, and sent the Fixer one offer: their debt, cancelled.

## Story audit

- **goal:** Recover the real stolen canvas as leverage to cancel the Fixer's debt.
- **stakes:** Their fingerprints and Jason's address will turn them into disposable suspects.
- **obstacle:** The auction is a staged frame-up; Jason wants to destroy the apparent prize.
- **planted clue:** Tomorrow's date in fresh ink on the bid sheet; painting addressed to Jason.
- **payoff:** An impossible high bid provokes the buyer to reach behind his chair, revealing where he concealed the original.
- **causal check:** Date creates suspicion; shipping label proves target; false bid threatens buyer's control; reflex exposes real prize; original buys debt cancellation, demand sent rather than magically accepted.
