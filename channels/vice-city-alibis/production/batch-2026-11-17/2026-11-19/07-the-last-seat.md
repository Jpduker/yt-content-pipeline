# The Last Seat

## Script

There was one seat on the getaway boat. Jason thought Lucia had finally decided what he was worth. Earlier, they'd stolen the Fixer's payroll chest. His men were closing the road. Lucia stripped the skiff's passenger bench while Jason hauled the chest aboard. He called her plan betrayal. Then he noticed fresh brass filings under the chest. Its padlock was new. Its hinges were not. The Fixer hadn't protected his payroll. He'd locked an anchor chain inside, threaded through the boathouse floor. Jason drove the loose hinge pins out. Lucia pulled the bench free, exposing their skiff's bolt cutters. They cut its thin joining link inside the chest, leaving the heavy chain and empty box behind. Lucia bolted the bench back. Two seats. No score. Jason sat down before she could change her mind.

## Story audit

- **goal:** Steal Fixer's payroll and escape by the established two-person skiff.
- **stakes:** Road closure forces water escape; Jason interprets missing passenger seat as abandonment, damaging trust.
- **obstacle:** Chest contains anchor chain tethered to boathouse floor; new lock conceals empty bait chest.
- **planted clue:** Fresh brass filings under new padlock, old loose hinge pins, chain emerging beneath chest; missing bench conceals useful storage access.
- **payoff:** Jason bypasses lock via old hinges, Lucia retrieves cutters; they sacrifice fake score and reinstall both seats before leaving together.
- **causal check:** Chain stays visible through loading and never teleports; cutter-sized thin link and inaccessible coil explain why lid must open; chest shifted by loading rail. Bench removal permits storage hatch access. All tools and boat established, no magic second boat or standing escape without seat. Chain is attached to chest interior lug via thin joining link; the external thick links and welded floor ring cannot be cut with onboard cutters.
