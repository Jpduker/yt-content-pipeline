# The Dead Frequency

## Script

The ransom call ended before anyone spoke. Jason thought they were bluffing. Lucia heard a weather report. They weren't buying a hostage. They were buying the recording that could put Jason away for murder. The seller demanded cash at the marina. Lucia wanted his transmitter. Jason called that an expensive guess. But the report described yesterday's storm. Their caller was broadcasting a loop, not waiting beside a hostage. They followed its strongest signal into the radio shop next door. The marina pickup was the ambush. Jason reached for the recorder. Lucia stopped him: the red light meant their footsteps were being transmitted. She held the instructions against the microphone and played them into the seller's own channel. The crew heard their boss demand payment twice. While they argued, Jason erased his voice. Lucia kept theirs.

## Story audit

- **goal:** Destroy the seller's unique original cassette that incriminates Jason while avoiding a cash handover ambush.
- **stakes:** Recorded confession puts Jason under the seller's control; marina meeting endangers both.
- **obstacle:** Jason favors paying; live transmitter would warn seller when they touch the deck.
- **planted clue:** Yesterday's weather report embedded in ransom call reveals prerecorded loop and nearby short-range transmitter.
- **payoff:** Replaying demands into live channel causes the waiting crew to question their boss while Jason destroys original tape; Lucia preserves crew replies as leverage.
- **causal check:** Local signal search narrows source rather than magically tracing a phone; broken service pane explains access. Physical original is destroyed, not an asserted deletion of every possible copy. Argument creates a short window, not permanent immunity.
