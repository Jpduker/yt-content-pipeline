# The Fixer Opened His Own Trap

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-17 story. Target: 56 seconds, 135 spoken words.

## Hook

## Exact narration

The warehouse gate slammed shut. Outside, armed guards blocked Jason and Lucia's van. The Fixer wanted everything. His gate-crew message gave the entry command: open the gate, let the van through. Lucia had saved it. Jason had laughed. Now the Fixer watched from an upstairs office behind smoked glass. He demanded their cash through Jason's private phone. Jason reached for his gun. Lucia held up her phone instead. She pressed its speaker against the gate microphone. The Fixer's own voice ordered the guards to open. One guard hesitated, looking upstairs. The office glass reflected neon; he could see no face behind it. The gate rolled back. Jason drove before the Fixer reached his office window to shout outside. Their cash stayed in the van. Jason asked why she saved every message. Lucia deleted his apology.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Get their cash van out of the Fixer locked yard without a losing gunfight.
- **Stakes:** Armed guards and a Fixer demanding the full score.
- **Obstacle:** Closed gate, guarded release and Fixer watching from private office, speaking only on Jason private phone; outside gate crew hears a separate yard PA.
- **Planted Clue:** A saved earlier entry-command message supplies the Fixer recognizable voice; smoked glass hides his reaction from his guards.
- **Payoff:** His own recorded order opens his trap; they leave with all their money.
- **Causal Check:** A brief labeled earlier insert supplies the recording rather than a miraculous voice clone. Lucia plays the existing command into a physical intercom. A guard chooses the normal gate switch; no remote hacking or instant forced lock.
