# Lucia Chose the Car Wash

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-17 story. Target: 56 seconds, 135 spoken words.

## Hook

Lucia drove their stolen coupe into a car wash. Jason saw the exit shutter closing ahead.

## Exact narration

Lucia drove their stolen coupe into a car wash. Jason saw the exit shutter closing ahead. The Fixer followed in his black sedan. He wanted the diamonds tucked inside Jason's zipped jacket. Jason shouted for reverse. Lucia pointed sideways: the staff door stood beside a delivery scooter outside. She stopped beneath the brushes. Behind them, the Fixer drove forward until his bumper blocked the entrance. Lucia pulled Jason through the staff door. Wet brushes swallowed their empty coupe and hid the movement. Jason grabbed the scooter. Chained. Lucia pulled a four-wheel delivery trolley beside it. Same downhill route. They rolled the trolley downhill, crouched inside, and vanished behind a laundry wall before the Fixer found daylight. On the level runout, Jason checked the diamonds. Still zipped. Lucia grinned. He had paid for the deluxe wash.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Escape the Fixer while keeping the stolen diamonds.
- **Stakes:** The Fixer is close enough to take the pouch if they stay inside the blocked wash lane.
- **Obstacle:** Closed exit shutter, pursuer behind and chained escape scooter.
- **Planted Clue:** Staff-door window establishes the sloping service alley, scooter and separate four-wheel trolley before the escape.
- **Payoff:** The car wash conceals an exit on foot; the unusable chained scooter makes Lucia choose the separate free-rolling four-wheel trolley and they retain the diamonds.
- **Causal Check:** Wash machinery blocks sight, the self-latching staff door buys a short lead, alley bollards prevent a quick car pursuit. Gravity supplies the brief downhill ride; no unpowered scooter magically starts.
