# The Bridge Would Not Open

## Exact narration

The bridge stayed down. Jason looked from their workboat to the cash beneath its light mast. Behind them, the Fixer's cabin cruiser rounded the bend. Lucia had refused that faster boat at the dock. Jason grabbed the wheel. Too shallow to turn. Lucia pointed at the light mast hinge beside his foot. He understood. Their boat could fold flat. The cruiser behind them had a welded roof. Jason released the brace while Lucia slowed the engine. The short light mast dropped toward their cash bags. He caught it across his shoulder. Lucia held their line. Bridge concrete scraped inches above his knuckles. They cleared the span. Behind them, the Fixer reversed; his cabin could never fit beneath it. Jason lowered the light mast. Every bag survived. Next time, he said, she could pick the boat.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Carry stolen cash through the canal before the Fixer catches them.
- **Stakes:** Losing the score at a low bridge with no turning room.
- **Obstacle:** Bridge fails to open, shallow banks block a turn, lowering light mast threatens the money.
- **Planted Clue:** Lucia chooses a slower shallow-draft workboat; hinge, brace and aft cradle are established before use.
- **Payoff:** Their folding light mast clears the span while the pursuer fixed cabin cannot; all cash survives.
- **Causal Check:** No unexplained drawbridge control or impossible boat transformation. The shallow-draft workboat has an established hinged light mast; engine is slowed before lowering; the pursuing cruiser roof remains rigid.
