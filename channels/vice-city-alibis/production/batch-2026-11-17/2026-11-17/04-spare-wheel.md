# The Money Was in the Spare Wheel

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-17 story. Target: 56 seconds, 135 spoken words.

## Hook

## Exact narration

Jason ripped open the courier bag. Newspaper. Outside, someone started dragging the warehouse shutter down. Lucia looked past the empty van. Mud covered every tire except the spotless spare underneath it. The Fixer had pointed at that bag too quickly. She shoved a dolly beneath the spare. Jason wanted the exit. Lucia cut the wheel loose. It landed heavily; folded bills bulged through a split. Then the shutter hit the dolly handle. Metal screamed. Their only doorway stopped two feet above concrete. Jason dropped flat and dragged the wheel under. Lucia followed, squeezing between the tire and that trembling shutter. The Fixer arrived holding the newspaper bag. Their getaway van outside was gone. His hidden fortune rolled away. At the lockup, Jason asked how she knew. Lucia tapped the clean rubber. Nobody washes the spare.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Recover a hidden courier cash shipment before the shutter seals the warehouse.
- **Stakes:** Leaving empty-handed or being trapped with the evidence.
- **Obstacle:** Decoy bag, concealed spare-wheel stash and descending shutter.
- **Planted Clue:** The spare alone is clean while every road tire is muddy; its abnormal weight confirms the suspicion.
- **Payoff:** They take the real money in the wheel; the Fixer is left holding his newspaper decoy.
- **Causal Check:** The dolly first carries the wheel, then its handle braces the shutter; Jason slides only the spare through the gap, with all road wheels staying on the courier van. Cash moves intact to the lockup.
