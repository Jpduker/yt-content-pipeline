# They Stole the Whole Desk

Status: PLANNED — new 2026-11-17 story. Target: 56 seconds, 131 spoken words.

## Hook

The cash drawer jammed. Jason had twenty seconds before the nightclub owner came back upstairs.

## Exact narration

The cash drawer jammed. Jason had twenty seconds before the nightclub owner came back upstairs. Lucia found the drawer bolted inside a steel desk. Jason reached for his crowbar. Too slow. She kicked the wheel brake. Earlier, the desk had rolled when Jason leaned against its edge. He stared at her. She pointed toward the freight lift. They were stealing the whole desk. The lift doors started closing. Jason shoved the crowbar between them while Lucia dragged their prize inside. Below, their van door caught the desktop. Jason swore. Lucia turned it sideways; the narrow end cleared. The owner reached his window just as his own office furniture disappeared into the van below. An hour later, the drawer finally opened. Cash everywhere. Jason kept one souvenir: the sign saying employees only.

## Story audit

- **Goal:** Steal the nightclub cash drawer before the owner returns.
- **Stakes:** Being caught inside the office with stolen cash and no time to force the drawer.
- **Obstacle:** Bolted jammed drawer, closing freight lift and van doorway narrower than desktop.
- **Planted Clue:** Desk rolls under Jason weight; caster brake receives its own readable insert before escape.
- **Payoff:** They remove the whole rolling desk, rotate it to fit the van, then open it safely later and keep the money.
- **Causal Check:** The desk stays intact from office to lift to van to lockup. Wheels solve weight, crowbar buys lift access, rotation solves width. One-hour end jump covers slow drawer opening rather than a magical lock bypass.
