A shot plan or generated contact sheet is preproduction, not final approved episode artwork. ## Visual execution

1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Use approved v2 faces first, then checked turnarounds and closets. Match Jason and Lucia to their reference files, not a fresh interpretation of their names. Keep action centered; reserve lower space for subtitles, avoid the bottom and right interface areas. Use 2–5 word caption groups, maximum two lines, warm ivory with dark edging; no wall of subtitles. Create captions from final audio alignment, not estimated script timings.

Eight images do not mean eight unchanging seven-second holds. Use purposeful detail cut-ins, reactions and small perspective-safe camera moves. No invented wheel rotation from still-image zooms. Establish geography before a chase. Show each prop's introduction, transfer and final state. Do not mirror faces or hands to fake new coverage. Verify five fingers only where visible, joined wrists, legible prop orientation, supported tires, matching rims/axles, valid seat positions and exact occupants. Check each full-size frame, transitions, and the entire final playback; automatic checks cannot guarantee no visual mistakes.

## Sound execution

Retain preferred Liam identity and approved settings from audio/voice-roster.json. Laura is approved as an alternative; Lara remains a requested candidate, not an approved generated voice. Dialogue casting is provisional and needs an audition before use. Native narration pitch/cadence, dry centered speech. Assign cues by actual beat: sparse curiosity; rising low pulse for suspicion; tight percussion and bass for chase; musical space for a reveal; brief warm or uneasy resolution. Match rain and engine perspective to interior/exterior, and siren distance to geography. No continuous sirens without police in the story.

Start music 14–18 dB below active speech, ambience 20–26 dB below, then listen. These are mixing starting points, not mastering guarantees. Duck backgrounds with smooth attack/release; let them recover in deliberate pauses. Aim around -16 LUFS for speech and at least 1 dB true-peak headroom on the final mix; measure the exported mix. Keep separate dry voice, original score, ambience, effects and mix stems. Do not reuse v3's four-section score indiscriminately.
