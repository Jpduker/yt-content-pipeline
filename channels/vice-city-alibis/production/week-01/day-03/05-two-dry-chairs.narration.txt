Two dry chairs stood in the shelter. Jason put their bag on one and guarded the other. Lucia watched rain spill through the doorway around a woman carrying a child against her shoulder. The woman asked for nothing. She stood beside a broken dryer, rocking slowly while the thunder moved closer. Jason tightened his hand around the sack. Every wet bill inside had cost them something they couldn't explain. Lucia lifted the bag off the chair. Jason looked ready to argue, then saw the child's bare feet. They stood by the door while the woman sat. Water from their sleeves gathered beneath the chairs. When daylight came, the woman thanked them for the seats. She never asked what was in the bag. Jason carried their money outside. Lucia carried the empty chairs back from the leak. Both finally felt lighter.
