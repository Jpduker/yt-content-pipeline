Jason paid for a box that was supposed to contain cash. Inside, Lucia found somebody's emergency instead. Sealed medicine packets filled the tackle box. A folded receipt named a clinic beyond the flooded road. Their seller was already gone. So was the money Jason had borrowed to make this deal happen. Lucia turned the receipt over. The delivery fee was unpaid. The medicine had never belonged to him. Jason stared toward the highway. He could still sell the box and recover something before morning came. Then the shack radio mentioned stranded patients. Lucia closed the lid, gently, as though somebody could hear. They left the box with a relief volunteer beside the public shelter, asking only for a receipt. Jason folded that receipt into his empty wallet. For once, proof of losing money felt worth keeping.
