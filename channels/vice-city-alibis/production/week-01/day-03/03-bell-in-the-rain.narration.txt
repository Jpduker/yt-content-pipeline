The bell rang three times from the empty pier. Jason thought their contact had finally arrived tonight. Lucia saw nobody behind the visitor counter, only an empty satchel somebody had carefully placed beneath it. A handwritten sign promised payment for bringing that bag to a waiting truck beyond the mangrove bend. Jason lifted the strap. The bell rang again, although both his hands were right there in sight. Lucia followed the sound upward. Rainwater filled a broken gutter, tipping a loose board against the bell. They had spent an hour answering weather. Their contact had never come. Neither had the promised money. Jason put the satchel back. Lucia tore down the sign before another desperate stranger could believe it. As they left, the bell rang once more. This time, neither of them turned around to answer.
