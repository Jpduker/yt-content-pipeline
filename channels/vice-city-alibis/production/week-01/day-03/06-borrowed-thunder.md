## Full script

I returned the money at dawn because a tree branch frightened me. Lucia won't let me forget. Hours earlier, three cracks hit the rental shed. I killed the lamp and crouched beside our stolen duffel. I wanted the back door. Lucia held my sleeve. Three cracks repeated, in identical rhythm. She pointed up. Wind bent a rotten branch against a loose shutter. I laughed before I could stop myself. Then a flashlight crossed the yard. An anonymous caretaker walked past us, close enough to hear us. He secured the branch and left. We stayed frozen beside the kayaks until his light vanished. Lucia switched the lamp on. The cash was still there. I couldn't remember what it was supposed to fix. At dawn, we left it for its owner. The branch hadn't threatened my life. It had shown me mine.
