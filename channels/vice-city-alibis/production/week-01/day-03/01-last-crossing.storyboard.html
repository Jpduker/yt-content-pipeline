The Last Crossing

Script

Lucia bought the last crossing, then watched the ferryman sell the same two seats to somebody else. The other passengers carried luggage. Jason carried an envelope that suddenly felt much too heavy for paper. Lucia checked the timetable. The last boat had left before they arrived. This one was staying tied. Across the creek, headlights appeared between trees. Their anonymous buyer had chosen a meeting with no exit. Jason reached for the money. Lucia reached for the waiting family's bags and carried them back uphill. The buyer flashed his lights, impatient. Jason finally understood: witnesses were supposed to leave on that boat. Lucia handed their fare to the family. Everyone waited together beneath the shelter until the headlights vanished. At sunrise, the real ferry arrived. They crossed without the deal. Jason called the lost money cheap.
