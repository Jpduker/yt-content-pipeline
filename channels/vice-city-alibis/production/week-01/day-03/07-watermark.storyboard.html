Script

I remembered every payment. The collector remembered the ones that let him charge us again. Rain had soaked his ledger before we reached the lodge. Our balance looked like a blue cloud. He tapped the blank space and demanded double. Jason spread the pages under the lamp, seeking our number. I let the man talk. Each explanation gave me time to find the entry he hoped I'd forgotten. Three pages earlier, my signature sat beneath a payment, dry. He had counted the same debt twice. Jason moved both entries into the light. The collector stopped explaining. I waited until he offered a receipt. He crossed out our balance and signed. We left his ruined ledger behind. I kept the receipt flat. Outside, Jason asked whether I'd remembered that payment. Of course. People always get generous when they call me forgetful.
