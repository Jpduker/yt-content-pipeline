Script

I stole a fake coat and left the real one. Jason thought rain had ruined my judgment. The real coat had a perfect label. The fake had crooked stitching and a sagging hem. Our buyer said the fee was inside a pocket. Both pockets were empty, Jason reached for his phone. I checked the claim ticket instead. It listed alterations, not storage, and charged for replacing six feet of lining. Someone had stitched our money into the hem. The bad label made sure nobody would steal it by accident. I took the seam apart with the booth's scissors. Six bundles slid onto the counter. Jason counted the fee twice. I kept the coat, because its supplier had taste in hiding places. Back outside, he offered his jacket. I told him mine had already earned more tonight than either of us.
