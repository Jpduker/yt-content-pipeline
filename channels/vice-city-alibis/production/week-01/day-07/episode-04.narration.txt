The billboard went dark at midnight. Jason got paid before it lit again. Lucia never touched the switch. Earlier, they'd found their debt agreement pasted onto a replacement advertisement, ready for morning traffic. The debtor threatened to display their names. Lucia recognized his signature below theirs, beside a larger amount. Jason wanted the paper burned. Lucia checked the lighting schedule: midnight blackout, then a paid advertisement at twelve oh one. They called no one. Lucia sent the debtor his own signed page, followed by the lighting schedule. He assumed they controlled the board. They only needed him to believe the darkness was his final warning. Payment arrived with twenty seconds remaining. Jason checked sender and amount before Lucia closed the screen. At twelve oh one, a perfume advertisement appeared. Their debtor had bought himself one very expensive minute of imagination.
