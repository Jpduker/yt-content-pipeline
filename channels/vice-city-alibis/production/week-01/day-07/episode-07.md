## Script

Their room receipt proved Jason was asleep during a robbery. Lucia remembered he hadn't slept in two straight days. Somebody had sold their alibi to another crew, using the motel's carbon receipt book. Jason wanted to leave town. Lucia held the receipt against the book and found the duplicate. The buyer had signed for that room, then crossed it out. Hard pressure left every letter underneath. They didn't need the crew's names. They needed whoever paid for two receipts for one room. Lucia matched the signature to their unpaid debt ledger. Their debtor had bought evidence that placed him beside stolen cash. She sent both pages. Payment arrived before dawn, with instructions to destroy the copies. Jason reached for matches. Lucia pocketed the book. Every score needs an alibi, she said. Ours apparently earns interest while we aren't looking.
