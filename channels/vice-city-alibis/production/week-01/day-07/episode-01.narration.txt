Jason returned to Vice City owing a ferry fare. Lucia returned carrying the fare box's only key. They'd stolen the key before leaving town. Tonight, the kiosk operator had replaced the collection box. Jason wanted to force it. Lucia noticed yesterday's ticket price still printed above a freshly painted price board. The box wasn't new. Someone had dressed the old one backward to hide the lock from passing thieves. Jason lifted the flap. Lucia reached underneath, found their keyhole, and opened the box without a sound. Inside, cash sat beside unpaid ferry stubs. The operator had been pocketing fares without issuing the matching tickets. They took the notes and bought two real tickets from the machine. Jason asked why she bothered paying. Because, Lucia said, tomorrow somebody counts passengers. Tonight, we're the only customers whose money actually reached the ferry.
