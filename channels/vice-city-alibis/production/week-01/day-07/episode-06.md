## Script

I rented a berth for a boat we didn't own. By closing, someone else paid our debt. Our buyer kept payment in a waterproof tube beneath the pier. The gate required a mooring receipt. Jason suggested swimming. I suggested paperwork, because the notice promised a refund if our berth was occupied. We paid at the machine. Then I photographed the buyer's skiff, blocking our rented berth. The receipt opened the gate. Jason collected the tube while I requested a refund at the kiosk. The kiosk charged the blocking boat's deposit and returned our rental. Our buyer had left his registration on the bow. Inside the tube, our fee was complete. Jason asked whether taking the skiff would complicate my arrangement. I said it would. Then I untied it anyway, and let him explain the paperwork while we left Vice City.
