The Washer Never Turned

2026-11-22 · 56s planned · 139 words · Narrator-led crime micro-story; close, dry narration.

Portrait shot plans — descriptions and staging, not generated final frames. Read with PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md.

Script

The washer ate Jason's token and never moved. Lucia heard it fall behind the wall. Their payment was supposed to emerge with a laundry sack. Its glass showed only dry cloth. Jason pushed another token toward the slot. Lucia stopped him and checked the service diagram beneath the lid. The washer had been disconnected. Its coin chute fed a separate locker, inside the detergent cupboard. Lucia opened the cupboard. The first token had released a small tray, but the drawer stayed caught on fabric. They eased the loose fabric from the drawer's edge. The snag vanished, and their sealed payment slid into view. Jason kept the second token. Whoever arranged this delivery had expected them to keep feeding the machine. Lucia taped the spare token above the slot. Underneath, she wrote: first load paid. They didn't offer a refund.
