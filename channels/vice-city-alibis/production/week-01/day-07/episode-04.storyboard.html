The billboard went dark at midnight. Jason got paid before it lit again. Lucia never touched the switch. Lucia recognized his signature below theirs, beside a larger amount. Jason wanted the paper burned. Lucia checked the lighting schedule: midnight blackout, then a paid advertisement at twelve oh one. They called no one. Lucia sent the debtor his own signed page, followed by the lighting schedule. He assumed they controlled the board.
