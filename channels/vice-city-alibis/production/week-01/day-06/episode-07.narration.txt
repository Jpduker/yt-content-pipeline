Jason pushed an empty wheelbarrow out of the quarry. Their buyer paid anyway, The mountain had kept the decoy. Earlier, they'd brought two locked cases to the weigh station. One held their score, the other their buyer's payment. The buyer insisted both cases weigh the same. Jason trusted the digital display until it flickered. Lucia read the mechanical dial underneath. Their buyer's case held stone, matched to the missing money. They left both cases on the slope and released the wheelbarrow brake under the buyer's camera. The cases rolled toward the crusher's loading trench. Lucia sent one message: transfer now, or neither case comes back. Payment arrived before Jason caught it. He took their real case, left the rocks, and opened the side gate. The camera saw empty hands and an empty barrow. Lucia carried their score behind the wall.
