Script

Jason sent the money downhill. The cable car came back empty, and Lucia smiled. Earlier, their buyer demanded delivery without witnesses. Lucia noticed the cabin's second floor hatch. The buyer controlled the lower station. He could take the bag, and cancel payment. Jason wanted to ride along. Lucia tucked the freight tag through the hatch and closed it on the ribbon. Downhill, somebody pulled the yellow bag out. The snagged ribbon tore cleanly, leaving half the weight. When the cabin returned, Lucia photographed the half tag against the empty floor and sent their buyer one message. Paid freight, confirmed received. The buyer's own insurance camera covered that floor. He transferred their fee instead of arguing. Jason had shipped cash. Lucia had shipped proof, and the proof came back on the return ride.
