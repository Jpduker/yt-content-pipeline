I bet our escape money against Jason. He thought I had lost faith. I had read the flag. The mountain race paid double if the lead car missed the overlook. Our buyer promised he couldn't lose. He'd fixed the timing board, bought the starter, and picked the route. He hadn't looked uphill. A rockslide had closed the final bend. The red flag meant the race would finish at the lower checkpoint. I put our envelope into the sealed wager box before the rules changed. Jason called me reckless. Then the lead car stopped below us. The overlook camera showed empty road, as my slip required. Our buyer couldn't dispute his own camera. He paid the wager, then asked whether we wanted the delivery. Jason said no. I said the odds were terrible, and we left with twice the promised money.
