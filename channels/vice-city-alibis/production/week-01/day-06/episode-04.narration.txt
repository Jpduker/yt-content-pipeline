Locker seventeen was empty. Lucia checked the key again, Their contact had rented them the wrong mountain. The map showed two lodges with identical lockers. One closed in summer; the other only opened in winter. Their contact had left no address, just a pine-stamped brass token with a horizontal scratch. Jason called it damage. Lucia turned the token over. The scratch crossed the tree at the old snowline. The winter lodge stood below that line. They followed the covered walkway instead of climbing another mile. Locker seventeen there had a coin slot. The brass token released it, and their pouch fell into Jason's waiting hands. Inside were their fee and the original key deposit. Their contact had paid for directions nobody bothered to read. Jason kept the money. Lucia kept the map, She would charge him for the scenic route.
