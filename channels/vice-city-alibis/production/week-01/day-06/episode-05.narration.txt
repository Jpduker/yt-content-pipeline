The ransom money was inside a cold stove. The note said light it. Jason reached for matches. Lucia stopped him. A sealed cash tin blocked the flue, turning the cabin into a trap. The note promised a key would drop when the stove warmed. Jason asked why anyone would ransom their money. Because they owe us more than this, Lucia said, and they need a reason we never come asking again. She checked the chimney outside. A fresh cap covered soot, fastened from the roof. Jason held the ladder. Lucia lifted the cap and pulled the tin upward, leaving the matches untouched on the table. Inside the tin, their cash wrapped the missing account book pages. The debt accompanied every delivery date. They took the money and the pages. Jason left the note inside the stove, where bad instructions belonged.
