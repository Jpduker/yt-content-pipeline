Script

Lucia stole eight pay envelopes. Every one was empty, but the payroll guard had signed for cash. Jason wanted the ledger. Lucia wanted the tray underneath it, where fresh pressure marks crossed yesterday's dust. She held an envelope against those grooves. Eight employee numbers matched eight signatures, all written by one hand. Jason said, Someone beat us here. Lucia shook her head. Someone was planning to blame tomorrow's shift. They could leave empty handed, or find the money before the missing wages became an official robbery. Lucia tipped the tray. Its weighted base split open, spilling rolled notes bound with identical employee numbers. They left the signed ledger beside eight empty envelopes, then carried the stolen wages through the loading exit. By morning, the payroll thief had his perfect robbery. Unfortunately for him, he'd signed every missing dollar.
