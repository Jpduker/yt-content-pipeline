The urn was supposed to hold diamonds. It held a receipt dated six minutes into the future. Jason called it a joke until Lucia found the delivery slot, still warm inside the cold warehouse. A courier had scanned the wrong package early. Somewhere behind that wall, their diamonds were still waiting. They followed a grinding belt toward a kiln, where rejected packages were dropped directly into the firing chamber. Jason reached for the emergency stop. Lucia caught his wrist. The stop switch also opened the disposal hatch. She fed the orange receipt through the reader instead. The belt reversed to correct its own failed delivery. The sealed urn rolled back to Jason, heavy this time. He bagged it without opening the lid. Their receipt showed one successful collection. The kiln's furnace log showed nothing missing, because nothing had burned.
