Script

White smoke poured from the wrong tank. Jason had one minute before their hidden cash went into the wash. Their money sat inside an inspection tin, wedged above the rinse line. Tonight's pressure test changed everything. Jason turned the nearest valve. The smoke thickened. Lucia grabbed the diagram and found arrows scratched through the labels. Wrong tank, she said. The replacement pipes cross underground. Close that one and you'll flush our money into storage. Storage meant an unreachable tank. Jason held still while Lucia followed the vent tube. She opened the inspection hatch, caught the tin as the pressure lifted it, and shouted for him to shut down. Smoke stopped. The tin stayed sealed. They left the valves as the diagram instructed. Their cash smelled like detergent. Jason called that clean money, and Lucia made him carry it back.
