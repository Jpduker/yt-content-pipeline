I sold Jason's key for five hundred dollars. Then the buyer locked himself inside our score. The door was supposed to open outward. Fresh hinges told me somebody had reversed it since inspection. Instead, I pressed the key into blue wax and took the offer. Our buyer wanted the cash box inside. He never asked why a storeroom needed a lock on both sides. When the ventilation quit, he offered double for an exit. Jason asked whether I had brought another key. I hadn't. I'd brought the locksmith's chalk, which marked the outside latch he'd forgotten to slide before entering. We opened up, took double payment, and let him carry away a box filled with scrap. I told Jason the key was worth five hundred. He counted fifteen hundred and finally asked about the chalk.
