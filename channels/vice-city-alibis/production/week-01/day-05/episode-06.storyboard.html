Script

I nearly bought the stolen tools back from myself. That's how tired I was. Jason had hidden our cash inside a tool chest. By sunrise, the refinery had impounded all three boxes. An auction stamp appeared on each inventory slip. The ink smeared on mine before anybody touched it. That meant the paperwork came first. Whoever stamped the slips hadn't opened the chests or looked inside. I asked Jason for the money envelope. He handed me an empty one and stopped looking nervous. We'd switched the labels while the ink dried. Our chest was now listed as damaged equipment, scheduled for disposal. We collected it at the scrap gate, paid the disposal charge, and recovered the cash beneath its false bottom. I lost twenty dollars on the fee. Jason called it expensive. I told him to buy his own money.
