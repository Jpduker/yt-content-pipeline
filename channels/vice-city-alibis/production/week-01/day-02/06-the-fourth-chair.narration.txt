I stared at the fourth chair. Nobody mentioned another person at our meeting. Jason and I had come to settle a debt. Our creditor placed four chairs beneath the motel awning. Three coffees cooled on the table. He kept checking the road. Jason counted the money again, quietly. I asked who else was coming. The man said his son. He wanted a witness when we paid him back. I thought witness meant leverage. Then he said his son believed every promise broke. Tonight, he'd show otherwise. We waited. A young adult arrived soaked and angry. Jason slid full payment across the table. He counted once, thanked us, and handed back our signed debt note. His son watched him do it. I took the fourth chair while they talked. We had arrived expecting somebody else to disappoint us that night.
