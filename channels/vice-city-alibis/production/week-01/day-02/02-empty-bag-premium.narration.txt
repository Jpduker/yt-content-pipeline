Jason delivered an empty bag and waited for accusations. The buyer hugged it before checking a single pocket. Lucia had insisted they leave the contents alone. There weren't any. That made Jason mistrust the job. Their anonymous client had offered real money for a worn satchel from a shuttered resort's lost property. At the desk, the buyer traced a crescent repair beneath its handle. His father had stitched that crooked seam. Jason glanced over. She had spotted the initials inside the repair and checked with the clerk. The bag had been held for years. Its owner hadn't known the resort was closing until morning. He paid their agreed fee and walked out carrying nothing else. Jason opened the envelope. Every bill was there. Lucia asked whether an empty bag still seemed worthless. Jason put their fee away without answering.
