The Borrowed Sunset

Script

Someone offered Lucia cash to remember a sunset she'd never seen. Jason thought yes would cost nothing. The man played a video: orange water, a blue railing, himself smiling beside an empty chair. He wanted her to say she'd sat there yesterday. One harmless sentence, he said, sliding over money. Lucia knew the terrace. Its railing had been repainted white last month. The man stopped smiling. Jason looked at the envelope, then video. Somebody else might pay for their convenient memory with years behind bars. He pushed the cash back. The man called them ungrateful and walked away without another word. They bought one coffee to share beneath the real white railing while rain erased the last orange light offshore. Jason raised the cup toward the empty chair. That one sentence, Lucia said, would have belonged to somebody forever.
