The seller gave Jason a name to trust. Lucia found it on a memorial before paying. They had left a deposit with the clerk for a boat neither had seen. Their seller claimed the owner was away. He said the key and title would arrive after the final payment. Lucia walked beneath the dock shelter to escape the rain. The same name shone on a brass plaque. The owner had died last winter. His boat had become a community rescue launch. It wasn't for sale. Jason called the seller. This time, the phone rang inside an empty tackle shop and nobody answered it. They took their receipt back to the clerk. She checked the listing, returned their deposit, and reported the impersonation. Outside, the rescue launch crossed the channel. Jason watched their imaginary getaway boat bring somebody safely home instead.
