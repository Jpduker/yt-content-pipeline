## Full script

Jason thought the driver had betrayed them. Why else stop the taxi with their destination still ahead? An anonymous man waited under the shelter, holding the envelope their buyer had refused. Jason reached for his door. The driver paused the meter, and pointed toward Lucia's seat. Her phone was lying beneath it. She'd dropped it. The waiting man was a dispatcher, carrying the envelope they'd left at the taxi office. Jason relaxed his hand. He'd been preparing for somebody to take something. Nobody had even tried. Lucia collected her phone. Jason checked the envelope in front of them. Their returned advance was still intact. He paid the fare and added a tip. The driver smiled and asked if they wanted their destination. Home, Lucia said. The meter started again. Jason watched its numbers climb and felt relieved to understand why.
