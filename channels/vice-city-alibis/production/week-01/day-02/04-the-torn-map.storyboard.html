The Torn Map

Script

Half a map cost Jason five hundred. The other half hung behind Lucia. A seller had promised buried money beneath an island house. The second piece would cost another thousand. Jason arrived at the land office to identify the shore. Lucia went looking for a restroom. She found the complete survey beside a drinking fountain. Their secret mark wasn't treasure. It was a septic tank. The seller had traced a public drawing and torn it to invent mystery. Jason stared, cooling. They couldn't recover the cash then. They could stop the next sale. Lucia photographed both drawings for the complaint. Outside, the seller called to ask whether they wanted the ending. Jason said he'd already found it beside a fountain. Then he bought Lucia dinner with what remained. For five hundred, he'd learned the difference between hidden and simply unread.
