## Full script

The suitcase was purple, just like the message said. The clown inside wasn't part of their deal. Lucia lifted a sequined jacket. Jason found giant shoes, stage makeup, and a drawing embroidered into the lining. They had taken the wrong case from a crowded depot, certain every purple suitcase belonged to their contact. A public announcement asked for missing theatre costumes. Tomorrow's children's performance had already sold every seat available. Jason checked the clock. Their real pickup window was closing. Lucia zipped the costume case shut anyway. They carried it to lost property, where a performer pressed both hands against the purple lid in relief. Their contact stopped answering. No case, no payment. Jason sat beside Lucia beneath the departures board, silent. Then he found one silver sequin on his sleeve. She grinned. First honest tip we'd earned all week.
