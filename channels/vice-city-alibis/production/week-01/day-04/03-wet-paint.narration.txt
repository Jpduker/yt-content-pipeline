Their getaway car came back with a blue door. Jason had paid for cream, and Lucia wanted answers. The painter shrugged and pointed at the invoice. One door replaced. Color matching had never been included. Jason called it a mistake. Lucia called it a landmark. Everyone they'd passed would remember this car. Then the painter mentioned the owner who'd collected his cream door yesterday, smiling through a mouthful of excuses. Lucia opened their envelope. The ownership papers were photocopies. Their car had been sold twice. Jason looked at the keys. The engine still worked. That made walking away feel harder than it should. They left the coupe with the shop and filed their claim from the bus stop across the street. The bus arrived blue and cream. Lucia laughed first. This time, Jason knew exactly what their fare bought.
