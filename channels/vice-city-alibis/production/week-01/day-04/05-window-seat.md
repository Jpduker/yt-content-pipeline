## Full script

Lucia picked the window seat so nobody could surprise her. Jason watched her reflection. The note on their table said someone inside knew what they'd done. Neither recognized a face there. A man beside the counter stood up. Jason tensed. The man only asked the waitress for extra napkins. Lucia saw headlights stop outside. Her coffee trembled. A delivery driver came in carrying a stack of bread. Finally Jason unfolded the note again. Beneath the threat, tiny print advertised the diner's murder mystery night tomorrow. Lucia read it twice. The waitress had placed one on every table, between the sugar and the ketchup. Jason started laughing, then stopped when Lucia didn't. She was looking at her hands around the untouched coffee. They paid and walked home together. Neither needed to explain why an ordinary invitation had sounded so believable.
