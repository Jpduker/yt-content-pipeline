## Full script

The buyer wanted midnight delivery. One minute past, he tried to keep Jason and Lucia's fee too. Earlier, Lucia had read the freight elevator's weight limit. The case could ride safely. Both couriers couldn't. Jason stepped out beside her. They sent the sealed case upstairs alone, then climbed the warehouse's stairs. Above them, the delivery bell rang. Through a window, Lucia saw the clock: one minute before midnight. They reached their buyer. His smile was enormous. Late arrivals didn't get paid, he told them. Lucia pointed at his delivery receipt. The building's caretaker had signed for the case when the elevator bell rang. He'd bought delivery, not their company. Jason held out his hand. The buyer counted their full fee into it. They took the stairs down together. Lucia kept the receipt. Jason complained that work had too many steps.
