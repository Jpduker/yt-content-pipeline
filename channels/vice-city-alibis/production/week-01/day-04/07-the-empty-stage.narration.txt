The key opened nothing in the cinema. Jason tried twice before Lucia read the ribbon. It carried a date, not a room number. Tonight. Their buyer promised a fortune for this key. They entered through the public side lobby, where a caretaker packed posters into a box. She recognized the ribbon immediately. The key belonged to a time capsule beneath the cinema's old stage. Jason asked about the buyer. The caretaker showed them a demolition notice. Someone wanted the capsule before witnesses arrived. Lucia set the key on the ticket counter. She asked when the neighborhood could come and watch it opened. At noon, the caretaker answered. Jason left their number for an invitation and tore up the private offer. The capsule held letters, not cash. They heard them read aloud the next day. Nobody had to steal those voices.
