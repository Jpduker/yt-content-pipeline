Script

Jason found the cash just as the container door slammed. Outside, Lucia heard the bolt fall into place. He called through the steel. His phone had no signal. Hers showed water rising around the lower hinges. The container sat below the pier, waiting for a crane that had stopped when the storm arrived. Jason stacked the money beneath the roof. Then he noticed an old tide stain above his shoulders. Lucia found the release bar bent under a fallen pallet. She could move the timber, or fetch help. She stayed. Jason shoved from inside while she dragged the pallet clear. The bolt finally slid back. He came out carrying nothing. Behind him, the floating cash spread across the container like expensive confetti. Lucia asked about the money. Jason pointed at the stain. For once, he had known when to quit.
