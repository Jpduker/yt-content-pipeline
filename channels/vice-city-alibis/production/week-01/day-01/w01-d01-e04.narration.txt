I grabbed the wrong bag. Same canvas, same zipper. Mine had been left under a motel chair. Lucia opened my prize. Toothpaste. Sandals. Receipts. She laughed until she found keys. The parking tag matched the car our buyer had arrived in. We had accidentally taken his overnight bag. Then my phone buzzed. He had my bag, and wanted twice the fee to give it back. Lucia held up his keys. Through the curtain, his car sat beneath a sign warning about overnight towing. I sent one photograph. Keys, parking tag, towing sign. No threats. Just three things he would recognize immediately. Five minutes later, my bag appeared outside our door. We placed his bag beside it, with everything inside. I checked the cash. Lucia checked my face. Next time, she said, buy a bag in another color.
