The Last Match

2026-11-16 · 56s planned · 140 words · Narrator-led

Portrait shot plans — descriptions and staging, not generated final frames. Read with PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md.

Script

Lucia burned their map at the waterfront. Jason watched their escape route turn black between her fingers. Earlier, he had found it tucked beneath their motel door. A shortcut through the cargo yards. The handwriting looked familiar. So did the coffee stain. This was the map they had lost. Someone had returned it with a new route drawn across the center. Jason nearly followed the fresh arrows. Lucia held it against the window. Original pencil marks continued underneath, past a gate marked permanently closed. The new route led them into that dead end. Whoever found the map was waiting for them there. Back at the waterfront, Jason unfolded the ferry timetable instead. Public lights. Open water. No shortcut through silent containers. Lucia dropped the last burned corner into a metal ashtray. They had lost a map, and kept their exit.
