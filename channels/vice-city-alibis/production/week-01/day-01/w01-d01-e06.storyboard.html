The Dead Man in the Shower

2026-11-16 · 56s planned · 140 words · Narrator-led

Portrait shot plans — descriptions and staging, not generated final frames. Read with PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md.

Script

The message said Jason was dead. Lucia heard him arguing with the motel shower through the bathroom door. A photograph followed. His boot beside a bloodstained sheet. The sender demanded cash to keep her name out. She remembered that boot yesterday. Someone had cleaned their old room after they checked out. He emerged carrying both boots, furious about cold water. Lucia held up the photograph. His anger became a grin. The sheet was theirs, too. He had spilled red sports drink while opening it with his teeth. Lucia photographed the very alive Jason holding both matching boots. Then she sent it back with one question. Would their photographer also bill them for laundry? The typing dots appeared, vanished, and stayed gone. Jason finally found hot water. Lucia put the cash away. Tonight, the only thing murdered was the shower pressure.
