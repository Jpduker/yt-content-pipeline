The Weight of a Promise

2026-11-16 · 56s planned · 140 words · Narrator-led

Portrait shot plans — descriptions and staging, not generated final frames. Read with PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md.

Script

Lucia handed Jason a thin envelope. He had crossed the waterfront expecting the money she promised. The storm had taken her bag, her phone, and every dollar from the job. She showed him nothing. He looked at the envelope. She had carried it dry inside her shirt, even after the bag disappeared. Inside was their ferry reservation, folded so tightly he had mistaken its edge for the lining. She had paid for both seats before the job. Losing the cash had not canceled their way out. Jason checked his watch. Boarding closed in minutes. He could demand his share, or take her hand. He left the envelope on the railing, then doubled back. Wind nearly carried their one useful paper away. Lucia laughed for the first time that night. They ran toward the ferry, broke together, and still going somewhere.
