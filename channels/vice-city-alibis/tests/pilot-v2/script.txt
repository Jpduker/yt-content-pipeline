Lucia found her own photo in their stolen car. Taken outside their motel... ten minutes ago.
Then their fixer called. "Bring the car to the docks. Come alone."
Under the seat: his cash bag... and a tracker. They were the delivery.
So Lucia sent the car ahead, on a tow truck.
At the docks, the fixer pulled a gun. The police were already closing in. Lucia had called them.
Across the water, Jason watched the lights. "There goes our payday."
Lucia opened the bag she'd taken from the car. "Your payday."
