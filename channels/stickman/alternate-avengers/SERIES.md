# Series direction and channel promise

## User decisions
- Keep **Between the Ifs** as the channel.
- Broaden the earlier relationship-only promise to include original ability stories and alternate superhero stories.
- Launch **Alternate Avengers** as a series alongside the original ability series.
- Imagined plots do not require film evidence. Present them as **unofficial fan fiction / speculation**, never as confirmed leaks or an official movie announcement.
- Use the mustard-jacket protagonist from the original B/C concept sheet, not the green-hoodie/black-headed protagonist for this series.
- Aim at B's illustrated expressive characters plus C's cinematic setting/lighting. Substantial art/rig development is authorized.
- Body motion is a primary quality criterion. Asset similarity and passing joint tests alone are insufficient.

## Broader promise approved in principle
**One impossible possibility. A whole story of consequences.**

Draft channel About (not applied to YouTube by Codex):

> What if the villain won? What if time stopped for everyone except you? What if one choice changed an entire universe?
>
> Between the Ifs turns impossible possibilities into animated stories—from extraordinary human abilities to superhero alternate realities and imagined futures.
>
> Familiar worlds. Unexpected choices. Consequences worth imagining.
>
> Our franchise-inspired stories are unofficial fan fiction and speculation.

## Content lanes to test, not a verified demand ranking
1. Superhero alternate outcomes: Doom wins, or saving a universe has an unexpected cost.
2. You inside the superhero world: the ordinary recurring protagonist witnesses or influences the impossible event.
3. Original extraordinary abilities with escalating consequences.
4. Villain viewpoints and moral dilemmas.
5. Time, memory and second-chance stories.

Niche, second-person framing, broad narrative structure and clear numerical premises can inform original work. Do not duplicate Ifverse's scripts, scenes, character assets, footage or sound. Original music does not clear someone else's protected artwork or film footage. Recognizable Marvel fan art also does not become rights-cleared merely because it is redrawn. The user authorized exploring unofficial franchise fan stories; this is a series-specific exception to older repo instructions excluding known characters, not permission to use unlicensed movie footage or scores.

Do not mix a children's-content experiment, the Whispers cast, or the original ability-series assets into this series.
