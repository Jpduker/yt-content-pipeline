# AA01 — What If Doom Was Saving the Multiverse?

**Status:** proposed first Fable test, not produced or user-approved as a finished script. Original unofficial Marvel fan fiction; no claim this is the movie's plot.

**Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, approximately 35–50 seconds as dictated by natural dialogue. Compose natively for vertical; do not crop the 16:9 workshop test. Working title: **What If Doom Was Saving the Multiverse?**

**Cast:** approved mustard-jacket protagonist + a stylized recognizable Doctor Doom fan interpretation in the same illustrated visual language. No actor likeness or voice imitation required. Use only two foreground characters; distant civilians may be simple atmosphere. New Doom assets belong under this series' assets/characters/doctor-doom/, not the channel's original character directory.

**Concept:** The alleged villain is holding a collapsing universe together. The protagonist gives up being remembered to save it. Doom alone remembers the sacrifice. A complete short emotional arc, with an impossible premise, visible cost and final payoff.

## Script seed — rewrite for natural performance
Narrator: They called Doom a monster. Then the sky split in two.
Narrator: You watched him hold your universe together with one hand.
YOU: Why aren't you saving everyone?
DOOM: I am.
Narrator: One person had to be erased from every timeline. You volunteered.
Narrator: The sky healed. Your friends walked past you. Your mother didn't know your name.
Narrator: Then Doom returned your watch.
YOU: You said everyone would forget.
DOOM: I didn't say I would.

Optional final on-screen question: **Would you still call him the villain?** Avoid padding the ending with a generic subscribe speech. This is invented fan fiction, not a theory supported by production evidence.

## Candidate visual beats
1. Immediate vertical hook: two skies collide, small hero below, Doom holding the fracture apart.
2. Establish hero/Doom eyelines, positions and screen sides; keep geography consistent.
3. Short exchange with distinct voices and restrained but legible acting.
4. The voluntary sacrifice: a watch/contact gesture with correct hands, grip and anticipation.
5. World repaired; show lost identity with a simple visual rather than a long exposition montage.
6. Doom returns the watch. Close-up of transfer: ownership changes once, no floating object, no extra hands.
7. Doom's last line and the hero's reaction; a quiet complete ending.

Demonstrate separate arm controls, correct palms, object handover, body balance and listener reaction. Preserve the hero's face and material appearance. Show the real motion clip and frames for review. Then refine, render the whole Short with music/SFX/voice/captions and verify the actual file.

No Marvel score or downloaded film montage. Use original/appropriately licensed audio. Keep generated fan visuals labelled unofficial and distinct from official footage. Do not publish to YouTube as part of this test.
