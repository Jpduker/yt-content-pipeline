# AA01 — What If Doom Was Saving the Multiverse? (final script v2)

Format: YouTube Short, 1080×1920 native, ~43 s. Unofficial Marvel fan fiction
(speculation), stated on the end card. Original visuals: the approved
mustard-jacket hero; the "Doom" of this story appears as an original
dark-sovereign figure (obsidian body, teal fissure light, no franchise
costume) — recognizably NOT the movie/comic design, named only in narration.
No franchise footage, score, or artwork.

Improvements over the seed: the hero volunteers in his own voice ("Take mine")
instead of being narrated into it; Doom's last line is tightened; the mother
beat is shown, not listed; one clean handover each way carries the emotion.
