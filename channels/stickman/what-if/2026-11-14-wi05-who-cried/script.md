# WI05 script: "What If You Could See Who Cried Because of You?" (v1, 2026-11-11)
Narration: second person, present tense, curious and detective-like at first, softer after the twist (Vivek, 2026-11-11).
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · MIRA · DRIVER bus driver · MUM. `(phone)` = on a call. `~ 0.8` = extra pause. `>` = stage direction.
Continuity: Mira moved away for her course in WI04 and is back for the weekend; the mum call in WI03 is one of the 41.

## HOOK
N: Today there's a small blue teardrop over some people's heads.
N: It takes you until lunch to work out what it means.
~ 0.4
N: They've cried. Because of you.
N: The bus driver has one.
N: Your mum has forty-one.
~ 0.5
N: Your best friend got hers… last night.

## ACT 1
> street, morning. Most heads are bare.
N: At first you think it's a trick of the light.
N: The man at the bakery, nothing. The woman with the dog, nothing.
N: The bus driver. One teardrop. And a look.
DRIVER: You.
YOU: Me?
DRIVER: Route nine. Six years ago. You paid in pennies. A hundred and forty of them. In the rain.
YOU: That made you cry?
DRIVER: It was my first day.
N: So that's the rule. A teardrop for everyone who's ever cried because of you. And a number, if it happened more than once.
N: Your old landlord. Three. You know why.
> insert: a drum kit in a tiny flat
~ 0.5
N: A stranger in the park. One.
N: You have never seen her before in your life.

## ACT 2
N: You decide to fix it. One apology per teardrop.
YOU: I'm sorry about the pennies.
DRIVER: Took you six years.
N: The teardrop stays. Apologies don't delete them. They just sit next to them.
> video call. MUM, 41.
N: Then, your mum. Forty-one.
YOU: Mum. I need to say sorry.
MUM (phone): What did you break?
YOU: Nothing! For all of it. I made you cry forty-one times.
MUM (phone): Forty-one? Is that all?
MUM (phone): Sweetheart. Half of those were your school plays. You were a very moving tree.
YOU: A tree.
MUM (phone): And the day you left for the city.
YOU: That one was sad crying.
MUM (phone): That one was proud crying. You'd know the difference if you called more.
MUM (phone): Like last week. When you called for no reason.
~ 0.6
N: You look at the number again. It doesn't say which kind.
N: It never did.
~ 0.5
> flash: two winters ago, a wallet handed in at a desk
N: The stranger in the park. Two winters ago, you handed in a lost wallet, and never thought about it again.

## ACT 3
N: Which leaves one teardrop. Mira's. Last night's.
N: She's back for the weekend. You thought dinner went fine.
N: You said her new place sounded small. You said the course sounded hard. You joked that she'd forget you by Christmas.
N: It has to be one of those.
N: You buy flowers. Then you decide flowers look guilty. You leave them on a bench.
> café
MIRA: You're doing the face again.
YOU: Okay. Did I say something last night? Something wrong?
MIRA: What? No.
YOU: You're sure? Because if I did—
MIRA: Why would you think that?
~ 0.5
N: You can't say, because there's a teardrop over your head. So you say the other true thing.
YOU: Because I'd want to know. If I ever made you cry.
~ 0.8
MIRA: Okay, that's unfair. How did you know?
MIRA: I found the card. In the box you packed. For when it's Monday and you hate it.
MIRA: I read it on my new kitchen floor, and cried like an idiot.
YOU: I forgot I wrote that.
MIRA: I didn't.
YOU: What did it even say?
MIRA: It said, you're allowed to hate it, and stay anyway.
~ 0.6
YOU: That's pretty good.
MIRA: Don't.
~ 0.8
N: The teardrop's still there. You hope it stays.
~ 0.6
N: In the café window, your own reflection. And over your head, small and blue,
~ 0.5
N: you know exactly whose that is.
