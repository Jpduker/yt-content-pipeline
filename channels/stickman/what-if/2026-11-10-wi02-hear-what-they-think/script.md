## ACT 2: the shortcut (work, lunch, train)

## ACT 3: the café (evening)
N: At six, Mira texts.
MIRA (text on phone, read by N): "Coffee? Rough day."
N: You get there first. Buy her favourite. Choose the quiet table. Rehearse three things to say.
N: She arrives without her usual yellow jacket.
N: You almost ask why.
MIRA-T: He's going to ask about the interview.
N: So you don't.
N: You talk about a film instead.
MIRA-T: Please don't make me do small talk.
N: You stop.
N: She fiddles with the cardboard sleeve on her cup.
MIRA-T: Say something.
N: You start.
MIRA-T: Not a joke.
N: You swallow the joke.
N: For the next ten minutes, you choose every sentence before she can dislike it.
N: You agree when she criticises the film you loved. Pretend you aren't hungry when she doesn't want dinner. Start laughing, then stop halfway through.
N: Mira watches you.
MIRA: You okay?
YOU: Of course. ✎ (spoken by the narrator voice, quieter: the hero has no separate voice)
MIRA: You're being really… careful.
N: Then you hear it.
MIRA-T: I miss him.
N: You look behind you.
N: Nobody.
N: She means you.
N: You're less than a metre away.
N: Your napkin is covered in instructions.
N: For the first time today, none of them helps.
MIRA: Did something happen?
N: You could invent the answer she wants.
N: Instead, you put the napkin between you.
YOU: I've spent all day trying not to annoy anyone.
N: She reads it.
MIRA: Even me?
N: You nod.
N: Her first thought is—
MIRA-T: That sounds exhausting.
N: The next is—
MIRA-T: I wish he'd trusted me.
N: That one hurts.
N: You leave it alone.
MIRA: I got rejected. From the course.

MIRA-T: Please don't say everything happens for a reason.
N: You put your hand back on the table.
YOU: I don't know what to say.
MIRA: Good. You can stop looking for it.
N: You sit together.
N: The coffee machine hisses. Someone drops a spoon.
N: For a few seconds, Mira isn't thinking about you at all.
N: Then—
MIRA-T: I'm glad he came.
N: You could tell her you heard.
N: You don't.
N: After a while, she looks up.
MIRA: I'm glad you came.
YOU: Me too.
N: You stay until the chairs go on the tables.
