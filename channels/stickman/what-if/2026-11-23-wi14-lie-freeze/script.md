# WI14 script: "What If Every Lie Froze the World for 5 Seconds?" (v1, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person, present tense. Built on P2 (#3 Short). Stake: Mum.
Rule (stated by 0:20): when anyone lies, everyone except the liar freezes for five seconds. Twist (Act 2): a kind lie freezes the LIAR instead, and only they don't know it.

## HOOK
> kitchen, night: Mum and Theo frozen mid-dinner, gravy in the air. Only you move.
N: Dinner. Mum is mid-sentence. Theo is mid-chew. The gravy is mid-air.
N: Because you just lied.
~ 0.4
N: In this world, every lie freezes everyone except the liar. For five seconds.
N: You told Theo you didn't eat his chocolate.
N: Five seconds is plenty of time to hide the wrapper.

## ACT 1
> office, morning
N: At work, it gets even better.
BOSS: Did you finish the report?
YOU: Yep.
N: Freeze. You don't finish the report. You do eat his biscuit.
> cafe
BARISTA: Is this one yours?
YOU: Yes.
N: It's not yours. It's bigger.
~ 0.3
N: Lying isn't a sin anymore. It's a superpower.
N: Five seconds at a time.

## ACT 2
> your flat, night: video call with Mira
N: Then Mira calls. Her course is going badly.
MIRA (phone): Be honest. Was moving away a mistake?
YOU: No. You're doing amazing.
~ 0.3
N: And this time, something is wrong.
N: Mira is still moving. She's waving at the screen.
N: You can't move at all.
MIRA (phone): Hello? You froze. Did you lie to be nice?
~ 0.4
N: A kind lie freezes the liar. And only the liar doesn't notice.
N: Which means everyone who ever lied to protect you froze in front of you.
N: You just never saw it.

## ACT 3
> Mum's house, Sunday afternoon
N: On Sunday, you visit Mum.
N: You ask the question you never asked.
YOU: Were you really okay, when I moved out?
MUM: Of course, love. Never better.
~ 0.3
N: And Mum freezes. Mid-smile.
N: You count all five seconds.
N: One. Two. Three. Four. Five.
~ 0.4
N: She's been freezing for years. Every phone call. Every goodbye.
YOU: You don't have to be fine, Mum.
MUM: ...I missed you. A lot.
N: Nobody freezes.
~ 0.5
N: Would you rather hear a kind lie, or the truth?
