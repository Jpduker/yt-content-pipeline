# WI19 script: "What If Your Dog Could Talk for 24 Hours?" (v1 full, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person, present tense. Stake: Biscuit, the family dog (11), and the family he quietly holds together.
Rule (stated by 0:15): every dog gets to talk once, for 24 hours. At midnight it stops.

## HOOK
> kitchen, 7 a.m.
N: At seven in the morning, your dog looks you in the eye and says:
DOG: We need to talk about the cat.
~ 0.4
N: In this world, every dog gets to talk. Once. For twenty-four hours.
N: And Biscuit has been waiting eleven years.

## ACT 1
N: First, the complaints.
DOG: The postman is a criminal. I've told you every single day.
DOG: And the vacuum. Why do you keep a monster in the cupboard?
N: Then breakfast.
DOG: Dry food. Again. Eleven years of dry food.
YOU: You eat socks!
DOG: Socks are a treat. There's a difference.
~ 0.3
N: By nine, Mum is laughing so hard she's crying.
MUM: Say something about Dad.
DOG: He sneaks me cheese. And he sings in the shower. Badly.
N: Dad goes very red.
~ 0.4
N: You take Biscuit to the park. Every dog is talking.
N: A poodle is reviewing the benches. A husky won't stop shouting.
N: Biscuit just walks next to you. Slowly.
N: Slower than last year.

## ACT 2
DOG: Can we sit down?
N: You sit. He climbs up next to you. It takes him two tries.
DOG: I need to tell you some things. While I still can.
N: The jokes stop.
DOG: When you were seven, you cried under the bed after school. Every day for a month.
DOG: I lay next to you. That was my job.
YOU: I didn't think you knew.
DOG: I always know.
DOG: Your dad cries in the car sometimes. Your mum talks to me when nobody calls back.
DOG: I hold all of it. I don't mind.
~ 0.4
N: Then he says the thing you weren't ready for.
DOG: My legs hurt now. The vet knows. You don't.
~ 0.6

## ACT 3
N: That evening, the whole family sits on the floor.
N: Nobody watches TV. Nobody checks a phone.
DOG: Mum. Thank you for the blanket by the radiator.
DOG: Dad. Keep the cheese a secret.
N: And then he looks at you.
DOG: You were my favourite job.
~ 0.4
N: At midnight, he stops talking.
N: He just thumps his tail. Twice.
N: You know exactly what it means.
YOU: I love you too, Biscuit.
~ 0.5
N: What would your dog say to you?
