# WI10 script: "What If Time Froze and You Could Wake Only One Person?" (v1, 2026-11-13, approved idea)
Narration: second person, present tense; playful and wide-eyed at first, then quiet and urgent.
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · LADY (a stranger, red coat) · THEO (mentioned) · MIRA (mentioned).
Rule (all stated by 0:35, never changed): at 18:02 on Thursday, the world stops. You can move; nothing else can, and frozen things can't be moved: they're like stone. You can wake exactly one person, once, by touching their shoulder. The freeze lasts one hour of your time. Then the world carries on exactly where it stopped, and only the person you woke will remember it.
Why not Mira: she lives four hours away by train (WI04) and nothing runs; on foot she is days away.
Motif: everything desaturated and blue-tinted; the one woken person turns back to full colour; a 60:00 timer counts down in the corner.

## HOOK
> cold open: a busy street at 18:02, completely still. Pigeons hanging in the air. Only you move.
N: At two minutes past six on a Thursday, the whole world stops.
N: Except you.
~ 0.4
N: You can wake one person. Just one. Touch their shoulder, and they're back.
N: You get one hour. Then the world carries on, and only that person will remember any of it.
~ 0.5
N: Who do you wake?

## ACT 1
> street, frozen. You poke a hanging pigeon. It doesn't budge.
N: The first thing you learn is that you can't move anything. A pigeon in the air is as solid as a wall.
N: The second thing you learn is that a frozen city is very, very quiet.
> café: a free-sample tray, frozen steam.
N: You eat a free sample. Then all of them. Nobody can stop you, and that turns out to be less fun than you'd think.
YOU: Hello?
~ 0.8
N: Fifty-two minutes.
> you walk, thinking.
N: The obvious choice is Mira. But Mira is four hours away by train, and nothing is running. On foot, she's three days.
N: Mum is an hour's walk. You'd get there just as the world starts again.
N: Theo is twenty minutes away, frozen on the sofa with a controller, mid-scream.
YOU: Theo it is.
N: You'd have a whole half hour of someone to talk to. You start walking home.

## ACT 2
> a crossing. A woman in a red coat, mid-step, looking at her phone. A cyclist frozen inches from her, not looking either.
N: Then you see her.
N: A woman in a red coat, stepping off the kerb, looking at her phone. And a cyclist, frozen a hand's width away, looking at his.
~ 0.6
N: You can't move her. You can't move the bike. When the world starts again, they meet.
> you check the timer. 31:00.
N: You stand there for a long time.
N: If you wake her, you walk home alone. Nobody will ever know there was an hour.
~ 0.5
N: If you don't, you'll know. For the rest of your life, you'll know.
> you touch her shoulder. Colour floods back into her coat.
LADY: Oh my goodness. Why is it so quiet?
YOU: Long story. Can you take one step back for me?
LADY: Why would I— Oh. Oh.
> she looks at the bike. She steps back onto the pavement.
LADY: That was going to hurt.
YOU: Yeah.

## ACT 3
> a bench by the crossing. Two people in a frozen city. 08:00.
N: You have eight minutes left. You spend them on a bench with a stranger.
LADY: Of everyone in the world, you woke me?
YOU: You were the one who needed it.
LADY: Who were you going to wake?
YOU: My brother. He'd have been annoying about it.
LADY: Then I'll be annoying about it for him. Thank you. Thank you. Thank you.
> 00:03. She takes your hand like an old friend.
N: Three seconds. She squeezes your hand.
> 18:02 and one second. Noise floods back. The cyclist rides past an empty kerb.
N: The world starts again. The cyclist rides past nobody.
> home. Theo on the sofa, losing his game.
THEO: Where've you been? You look weird.
YOU: I had a quiet hour.
THEO: Lucky.
N: Nobody else will ever know. One woman in a red coat will remember it forever.
