## P3 · Midnight Deletes the Person You Ignored Longest · opening A (rule first) · ≈ 39 s
**Outcome:** bittersweet, a little unsettling; both lose the contact, they keep the call.
**Titles:** ① Midnight Deletes the Person You Ignored Longest (47) ② 41 Days Without Calling Her Dad. Then Midnight. (47) ③ Her Phone Deletes Whoever She Ignored Longest (45)
