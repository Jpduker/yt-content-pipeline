# O26 · Every Parent Gets 3 Rewinds a Year. Mum Used Hers on Me. (opening B)
## HOOK
N: In this world, every parent gets three rewinds a year. Ten seconds each.
N: This year, Mum used all three on me.
## ACT 1
N: Rewind one. January. I drop her birthday cake.
N: Click. I catch it.
N: Rewind two. June. I shout that I hate her.
N: Click. I never said it.
N: Rewind three. September. I'm leaving for uni.
N: She hugs me goodbye. Then the world clicks back ten seconds.
N: And she hugs me again.
MUM: I didn't fix anything. I just wanted that one twice.
