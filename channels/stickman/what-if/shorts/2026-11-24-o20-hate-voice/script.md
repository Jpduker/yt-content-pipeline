# O20 · Her Voice Disappeared After She Said "I Hate You" (opening B)
## HOOK
N: Nina said I hate you to her brother. And her voice disappeared.
N: In this world, those three words take your voice. Until you mean the opposite.
## ACT 1
N: She tries to say sorry. Nothing comes out.
N: Three days. Not one word.
N: Oli pretends not to care.
N: On the fourth day, Oli falls off his bike.
NINA: Oli!
N: Her voice comes back. With his name.
