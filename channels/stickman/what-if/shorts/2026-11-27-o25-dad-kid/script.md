# O25 · In This World, Dads Turn Into Kids When They Laugh (opening A)
## HOOK
N: In this world, when a dad laughs, he turns into a kid. For one minute.
N: Your dad hasn't laughed in two years.
## ACT 1
N: Not since he lost his job. He just fixes things. Quietly.
N: So you try everything. Bad jokes.
YOU: Why did the scarecrow win an award? He was outstanding in his field.
N: Nothing.
N: Then you find his old football in the shed. You kick it at him. Badly.
N: It hits him right on the head.
N: And he laughs.
N: Suddenly he's eight. Gap teeth. Grass on his knees.
KID: Again! Kick it again!
N: For one minute, you play with the boy your dad used to be.
N: When he grows back, he's still smiling.
DAD: Same time tomorrow?
