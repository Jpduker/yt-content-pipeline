# O23 · In This World, You Can Borrow 1 Day From Your Brother (opening A)
## HOOK
N: In this world, you can borrow a day from your brother. He loses it. You get two.
N: Rafa borrows days all the time. Exams. Dates. A lie-in.
## ACT 1
N: His little brother Tom never says no.
N: Today, Rafa finds Tom's calendar.
N: Forty-two crossed-out squares.
N: His school play. His birthday party. Sports day.
N: Rafa doesn't borrow a day. He gives one back.
TOM: Wait. You can do that?
RAFA: Apparently. Pick a day.
