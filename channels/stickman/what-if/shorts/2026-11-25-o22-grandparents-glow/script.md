# O22 · In This World, Lonely People Glow (opening B)
## HOOK
N: Jun's nana glows every night. Everyone on the street can see it.
N: In this world, lonely people glow.
## ACT 1
N: Jun walks past her window every day on the way to football.
N: Tonight, he knocks.
NANA: Jun! Come in, come in.
N: Two hours. Biscuits. Old photos.
N: When he leaves, her window is dark. In a good way.
N: Then he notices the house next door. Glowing.
