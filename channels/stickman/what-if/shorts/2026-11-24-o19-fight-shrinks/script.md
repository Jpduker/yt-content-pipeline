# O19 · In This World, Every Sibling Fight Shrinks the House (opening A)
## HOOK
N: In this world, every time siblings fight, the house shrinks a little.
N: Leo and Maya fight a lot.
## ACT 1
MAYA: That's my charger!
LEO: You stole it first!
N: The hallway loses a metre.
N: By Friday, they share one tiny room.
N: Their knees touch. Their heads touch the ceiling.
LEO: Sorry about the charger.
MAYA: Sorry about your hoodie.
N: The house sighs. And grows back. Just a little.
