# O27 · In This World, Your Shadow Stays With the Last Person You Hurt (opening A)
## HOOK
N: In this world, your shadow doesn't follow you. It stays with the last person you hurt.
N: This morning, mine left.
## ACT 1
N: It's standing next to my little sister, Ava. She won't look at me.
N: Yesterday I laughed at her drawing. In front of my friends.
N: She said it didn't matter. Shadows don't lie.
N: All day, it stays with her. At breakfast. On the bus. At her desk.
N: So I knock on her door.
YOU: Your drawing was really good. I was being an idiot.
N: She shrugs. Then she holds up a new one.
N: It's me and her. Walking home. And my shadow, right behind me.
