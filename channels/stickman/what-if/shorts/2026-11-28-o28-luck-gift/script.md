# O28 · Her Brother Had 24 Hours of Luck. He Gave It Away. (opening B)
## HOOK
N: Once in your life, you get twenty-four hours of perfect luck.
N: Oli got his on a Tuesday.
## ACT 1
N: He found twenty euros on the pavement. Every light turned green.
N: The vending machine gave him two drinks.
N: Then his sister Nina called him. Crying.
N: Her job interview was at three. She was sure she'd fail.
N: In this world, luck can be given away. Once. With a hug.
N: So Oli runs across the city.
OLI: Come here.
N: At three, Nina walks in. At four, she has the job.
N: Oli trips on the way home. Twice.
OLI: Best Tuesday ever.
