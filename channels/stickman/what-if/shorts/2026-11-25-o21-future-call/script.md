# O21 · Every Birthday, You Get 1 Call From Your Future Self (opening A)
## HOOK
N: In this world, on every birthday, you get one call from your future self.
N: Ava's future self always says the same three words.
FUTURE (phone): Call Grandad more.

N: At twenty, she finally listens. She calls him every Sunday.
N: At twenty-five, the call comes again.
FUTURE (phone): Hi. I've got nothing to tell you this year.
FUTURE (phone): You already did it.
