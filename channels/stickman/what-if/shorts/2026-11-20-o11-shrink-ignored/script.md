# O11 · "In This World, You Shrink When You're Ignored" (original Short, opening A = rule first; teaser for WI11 the same evening) · v1 2026-11-13
Third person. Tags: N · PIP (office worker, new, pink cardigan) · CLEANER (night cleaner, new, mop).
Rule (stated by 0:08, never changed): every time someone looks straight through you, you shrink a little. When someone really sees you, you grow back.
Outcome: unsettling then sweet: she finds a whole hidden community of tiny people, and becomes the one who notices.

## HOOK
First frame (not a beat): an office; a woman the size of a coffee cup standing on the photocopier, waving; nobody looks.
N: In this world, when people look straight through you, you shrink.
N: By Thursday, Pip is the size of a coffee cup.
## ACT 1
> Pip waving from the photocopier; colleagues walking past.
PIP: Hello? I'm on the photocopier.
N: Nobody notices. Somebody photocopies her shoe.
## ACT 2
> night. The office dark. A cleaner with a mop stops.
CLEANER: Well, hello. You're very small.
PIP: You can see me?
CLEANER: Course I can. Nobody sees me either. Want a lift?
> Pip on his shoulder. She grows a little, the size of a cat.
N: Pip grows a little. The size of a cat.
## ACT 3
> under the desks: tiny people, a whole little town of the overlooked, waving up.
CLEANER: You're not the only one.
N: Under the desks, there's a whole town of people nobody looks at.
N: Pip says hello to every single one. They all grow.
