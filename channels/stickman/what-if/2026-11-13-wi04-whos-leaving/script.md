# WI04 script: "What If You Could See Who's About to Leave Your Life?" (v1, 2026-11-11)
Narration: second person, present tense, crisp and ticking like a clock; dialogue carries the scenes (Vivek, 2026-11-11).
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · MIRA · NEIGH neighbour · COLL colleague · BARISTA. `(text)` = a sent message. `~ 0.8` = extra pause. `>` = stage direction.

## HOOK
N: One morning, everyone you know has a number over their head.
N: Your barista. Two thousand and ninety.
N: Your boss. Four hundred and twelve.
N: Your best friend.
~ 0.55
N: Three.
~ 0.5
N: It doesn't say why. It only says how long.

## ACT 1
> front steps. The neighbour's number is 0. A van idles.
N: You don't know what it's counting. Not yet.
N: Your neighbour's says zero.
NEIGH: Morning! Well. Last morning. Van's here.
YOU: You're moving?
NEIGH: To the coast! Did I not tell you?
N: Zero. And he's gone.
N: So that's what it counts. Days. Until someone leaves your life.
> office
> café counter
BARISTA: Usual?
YOU: You're going to be here a while.
BARISTA: Thanks?
> office
N: At work, you check everyone.
N: Your boss, four hundred and twelve. You do the maths. A promotion, probably. His.
N: Your colleague. Twelve.
YOU: You're quitting.
COLL: What? Who told— I haven't even told my mum.
N: The office plant. Four. Nobody's watering it.
N: Your gym says zero. Fair.
~ 0.4
N: But you keep coming back to three.

## ACT 2
> café. Mira, yellow jacket, a 3 over her head.
MIRA: You're staring.
YOU: Am I? Your hair looks nice.
MIRA: It's the same hair.
N: Three days. So you do what anyone would. You make yourself impossible to leave.
N: Her favourite coffee. Every morning.
N: The shelf she asked about in March.
N: The story about her cousin. You laugh at all of it. Again.
MIRA: Okay. Who are you, and what do you want?
YOU: Nothing! Can't a friend fix a shelf?
~ 0.4
N: That night, you check.
~ 0.4
N: Two.
> day two, dinner at her place
MIRA: Hey. Can I tell you something?
YOU: First, dessert! I got the one you like.
~ 0.5
MIRA: Sure.
N: She was about to say something. You gave her cake instead.
N: It isn't counting how she feels. It's just counting.
> night, bedroom, phone scroll
N: So you start guessing. A fight you haven't had yet. Something a doctor said. Something you said.
N: You read every message you've sent her since spring.
~ 0.5
N: One.

## ACT 3
> her hallway. One cardboard box. Then six.
N: You find out by accident.
YOU: Mira. What's all this?
MIRA: Okay. Don't be mad.
MIRA: I got in. The course. The one that said no last year.
YOU: Mira, that's huge!
MIRA: It's four hours away. It starts Monday.
~ 0.5
YOU: And you didn't tell me.
MIRA: I tried! Like, nine times. But you were being so nice. And I thought, if I tell him, he'll be nice about that too. And I'll never know what he really thinks.
~ 0.6
YOU: I think…
N: Her number flickers. One. Nine hundred. One.
N: If you ask her now, she stays.
~ 0.9
YOU: I think you're going to need more tape.
MIRA: That's what you think?
YOU: And I think I'm going to hate Mondays.
~ 0.5
> packing
MIRA: Why are you wrapping one mug in three jumpers?
YOU: It's your favourite mug.
MIRA: It's your mug. I stole it in March.
YOU: Then it's coming with you.
~ 0.5
N: You carry the heavy box. She pretends not to notice you picked the heavy one.
> train platform, dusk. 0 over her head.
N: Zero is a train platform.
MIRA: Don't make it weird.
YOU: I'm not making it weird.
MIRA: You're doing the face.
YOU: Text me when you get there.
MIRA: It's four hours. Not the moon.
YOU: Then text me from the moon.
~ 0.8
N: The doors close. The number goes out like a light.
~ 1.0
> phone buzzes
MIRA (text): Seat fourteen. Already bored. Call you tonight?
~ 0.5
