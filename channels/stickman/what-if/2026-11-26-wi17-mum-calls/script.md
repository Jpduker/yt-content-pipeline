# WI17 script: "What If You Aged 1 Year Every Time You Ignored Mum's Call?" (v1, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person. Stake: Mum. Ages shown as a counter over the hero (he stays himself: same face, same hoodie).
Rule (by 0:15): every call from Mum that you ignore makes you one year older. It never reverses.

## HOOK
> your flat, Friday: the hero stiffly getting up, counter 28
N: On Monday you were twenty-three. It's Friday, and you're twenty-eight.
N: Because you ignored five of Mum's calls.
N: In this world, every call from your mum that you ignore makes you one year older.

## ACT 1
> office, football pitch, café
N: It started small. A missed call in a meeting. Twenty-four.
N: A call during football. Twenty-five.
N: Her birthday. You were busy. Three calls. Twenty-eight.
N: Your knees click now. Your back hurts when you stand.
N: Your friends think you've been working too hard.

## ACT 2
> your flat, a week later
N: Then the calls stop.
N: A whole week. Not one.
N: You should feel relieved. You're not getting any older.
N: But your phone has never been this quiet.
N: So you call her. It rings. And rings.
N: Voicemail.
~ 0.6

## ACT 3
> train, then Mum's garden
N: You take the first train home.
N: Mum is in the garden. Her phone is in the kitchen. On silent.
MUM: Oh! I didn't want to bother you anymore.
YOU: Bother me. Please. Every day.
~ 0.4
N: You don't get any younger. That's not how it works.
N: But from now on, you pick up on the first ring.
N: When did you last answer on the first ring?
