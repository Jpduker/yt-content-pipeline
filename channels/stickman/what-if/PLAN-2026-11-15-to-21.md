## 1. The rules every Short follows from now on (from our 4 Shorts + Studio notes)
1. **Frame 1 = the stakes.** On-screen premise text from the first frame (e.g. "DAY 1 OF 3 TO MAKE HER STAY", "1,000 WORDS LEFT TODAY") + the counter/badge already visible. No chit-chat, no establishing wide.
2. **Hook line ≤ 1.5 s**, and the title says the same thing (title = first line).
3. **25–35 s** (our 30 s Short held 73.6%; the 41 s one 64%).
4. **End on a question with two answers**, on screen for 1.5 s ("Would you ask her to stay? YES / NO") + the same question as the pinned comment. Vivek/JP answer every comment in the first hour.
5. **Loop**: the last line or image leads back into frame 1.
6. Cut Shorts from a long-form end with the question, then 1.2 s "Full story on the channel".
7. Titles: a number + a deadline, or "In This World, <rule>". ≤ 50 characters.

## 5. The 14 original Shorts (one-line treatments; scripts next)
**"In This World…" (S3)**, each 25–35 s, new tiny cast, ends on a two-answer question:
- 1,000 Words a Day: a counter over every head; he wastes 990 arguing online; with 2 left he calls Mum: "Love you." → "What would you save your last words for?"
- "I'm Fine" Turns You Grey: a whole office of grey heads; one woman says "I'm not fine, actually" and her colour comes back. → "How grey would you be?"
- Every Promise Is a String: strings between wrists; one snaps when a dad misses a recital; he ties it again in the car park. → "Would your strings hold?"
- Every Compliment Is a Coin: the kind are rich, the rude are broke; a kid gives every coin away and the town's poorest man starts paying it forward. → "Would you be rich here?"
- Umbrellas Only Open If You're Loved: a man stands soaked; his umbrella opens when his dog runs out to meet him. → "Would yours open?"
- You Shrink When You're Ignored: a cup-sized colleague nobody sees; one hello and she's full size. → "Who's shrinking around you?"
- Your Shadow Shows What You Want: a banker's shadow plays guitar; a kid's shadow is a hug. → "What would your shadow do?"
**Number/deadline (S4):**
- Gravity Stopped for 5 Seconds at Work: 5 → 0 counter, coffee, the boss, the photocopier; you catch the intern. → "What would you grab?"
- Your Phone Deletes a Contact Every Day You Don't Call: Day 1… Day 6; Mum's name blinks at the top. → "Who would you call first?"
- He Had 60 Seconds to Tell Everyone the Truth: a timer; barista, stranger, boss; the last 3 seconds for his dad. → "Who gets your last 3 seconds?"
- Time Stops for 5 Seconds Every Time You Lie: comedy of freezes; then he lies "I'm okay" and gets 5 seconds to think. → "Would you lie less?"
- You Have 10,000 Steps Left: the counter ticks with each step; he stops taking the stairs, then walks 3 km to a friend. → "Where would you walk?"
- He Got 3 Wishes. His Enemy Got Each One Twice: he wishes for money, fame, then "half a broken heart". → "What's your third wish?"
- He Had 7 Days to Make His Grandpa Laugh: Day 1 jokes fail; Day 7 old photos; Grandpa laughs at the photo, not the joke. → "What makes your grandparents laugh?"
