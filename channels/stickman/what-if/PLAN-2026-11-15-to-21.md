## 2. Rules for every Short this week (replace v1 §1)
**Opening spec** (written out per Short, see §5): exact first frame · overlay (≤ 5 words) · first spoken sentence · what changes on screen in the first 3 s. [choice] The rule must visibly affect someone inside the first 3 s; a premise banner over a still character doesn't count.
**Title and opening make the same promise, not the same words.** No long sentence crammed into 1.5 s.
**A whole miniature story:** a clear rule → a choice with a cost → escalation → an earned payoff. Cuts from a long-form are rebuilt for someone who never saw it (new first line and frame where needed).
**Length:** 25–35 s as a working range, set from the real voiceover timing (pauses and reactions included), not forced.
**Ending:** payoff first, nothing after it except optionally a short question over the final action (≤ 2 s on screen). [choice] For this week: a question over the final action for every Short, identical style, plus the same question as the pinned comment. **Removed:** the separate question card and the "Full story on the channel" card. Use YouTube's **Related video** to link the long-form where one exists.
**Questions about the character's decision**, not YES/NO ("Would you have called him first?").
**Loop:** optional; only when the last frame naturally leads back to the first without confusing the ending.
**Vary outcomes:** across the week, no two Shorts in a row end on the same note. Banned as a default ending: "call Mum / say love you / notice someone". Mix humour, sacrifice, regret, reconciliation, unsettling, imperfect choices.
**Cast:** recurring characters (hero, Mira, Theo, Mum) keep their approved faces and colours, but each Short works without the long-forms. The hero never changes outfit.

## 4. Experiment: opening A vs B (directional, not a controlled test)
- **A · rule first:** the first line states the rule while it's already visibly affecting someone.
- **B · consequence first:** the first frame shows something impossible happening; the rule is explained within ~4 s.
- Held roughly constant: original third-person stories, same narrator voice, 25–35 s, same ending treatment, posted in the morning slot or alternating so each approach gets both slots.
- By Sat: about 5–6 of each among the 11 originals. Cuts are reported separately, not counted in A/B.
- No near-identical duplicates uploaded to test hooks.

### 6.3 Decision rules (ours, not universal thresholds)
- Compare **medians** of comparable uploads (A vs B originals; cuts separately), never one video against another.
- A Short with fewer than ~1,000 feed impressions at the 72 h read is **inconclusive** and isn't counted.
- Repeat an opening approach if, at 72 h, its median stayed-to-watch is ≥ 5 points higher across ≥ 4 conclusive Shorts each **and** its payoff retention isn't lower. Otherwise keep testing.
- Repeat a premise type (rule world vs number/deadline vs "5 seconds") the same way, once it has ≥ 3 conclusive Shorts.
- Long-form: after 7 days, look at CTR by source and 0:30 retention before changing cadence.
- No promises about virality or monetisation by a date.
