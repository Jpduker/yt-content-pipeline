## Logline
You can see every text people almost sent you. You answer them, people pull back, and at coffee your best friend's 3 a.m. "I'm not okay" is still flickering. You delete your reply and give her the room to say it herself.
Tone: light comedy → unease → tenderness; narration confessional and quiet. Hero = "YOU", second person, green hoodie always (RULES).

## Sets

## Motif (the rule on screen, the same way every time)
Ghost bubble: grey, dashed, flickering; typed word by word as the sender's voice reads it, then deleted. Phone inserts: pile (+ UNSENT counter), typing (thumb on send → backspace), real (solid blue = sent). Unsent texts are not in the caption bar.

## Direction rules applied (HANDOFF §6)
- Every emotional or reactive line is a close-up (bust) or a medium (cam z ≥ 1.6); wides only establish or stage comedy.
- Expressions are keyed to words and lead the line by 0.3 s; 2–3 step arcs per shot; a reaction shot after hurtful or tender lines.
- Slow push-ins on the emotional beats; hero left facing right in every conversation (180° rule).

## Music plan
