# WI18 script: "What If a Bell Rang the Last Time You'd See Someone?" (v1, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person. Stake: Theo. Twist: the bell means the last time you see someone *there*; Theo is moving two streets away (into Mira's old flat, canon WI04/WI16).
Rule (by 0:15): a bell rings the last time you'll see someone. Everyone hears it. Nobody knows what comes after.

## HOOK
> your flat, morning: Theo walks out with a bag; a small golden bell rings over the door
N: Theo walks out of the door. And a bell rings.
N: In this world, a bell rings the last time you'll see someone.
N: Everyone hears it. Nobody knows what comes after.
