## 3. What changes from batch 1 (v3 → v3.1)
### 3.1 Shorts hooks (from YouTube Studio, WI03 Short 2 after ~5 h: 43 views, 20% viewed vs 80% swiped, 64% average view duration)
The story held people who stayed; the first second lost 4 in 5. That Short opened mid-story ("At work, it turns out to be useful") with no premise on screen.
- **Every Short opens on its premise in under 1.5 s**: a Short-only hook line (narrator, ≤ 2.5 s, synthesised separately) over the strongest picture of the rule (the badge, the bubble, the shimmer), plus the premise as on-screen text for the first 2.5 s.
- **Frame 1 already shows the conflict** (no establishing wide, no fade-in, no title card).
- **The Short's title = its first spoken line**, near enough word for word.
- Cut points: each Short is one self-contained mini-arc (rule → escalation → sting), 30–40 s, ending on a line that makes the long video the payoff.
- Tool change: `make_short.py --hook "line" --hookText "TEXT"` (synthesise with build_voice's narrator settings, prepend 0.15 s before the first segment, burn the premise banner at ~20% H in the Short's caption font). Watch "viewed vs swiped" per Short; target ≥ 40%.
