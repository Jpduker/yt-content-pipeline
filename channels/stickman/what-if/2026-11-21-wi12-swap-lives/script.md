# WI12 script: "What If You and Your Best Friend Swapped Lives for a Day?" (v1, 2026-11-13, approved idea)
Narration: second person, present tense; comic and quick, warm at the end.
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · MIRA (phone) · PROF (Mira's course leader, new) · GROUP (Mira's course mates: AMA, new) · THEO.
Rule (all stated by 0:25, never changed): from midnight to midnight, you and Mira live each other's lives: her flat, her phone, her day; she gets yours. Everyone else sees you as each other and finds nothing strange. You keep your own minds. At the next midnight, everything swaps back.
Callback: WI03, the forty unsent messages in your drafts.
Picture: we always see the hero as himself (channel rule); other characters treat him as Mira. A small yellow "MIRA" tag floats over him while swapped, a green "YOU" tag over her.

## HOOK
> cold open: you wake up in a small flat you don't recognise. A yellow scrunchie on the pillow.
N: You wake up in Mira's bed. Four hours from your own.
N: Her phone. Her flat. Her alarm, which is a song you hate.
~ 0.4
N: For one day, you're living her life. And she's living yours.
N: Everyone else thinks this is completely normal.
~ 0.4
N: The swap ends at midnight.

## ACT 1
> phone call: Mira in your flat, Theo in the background.
MIRA (phone): Your brother is eating cereal out of a pan.
YOU: We don't have clean bowls.
MIRA (phone): Why do you live like this?
YOU: What's on your calendar today?
MIRA (phone): Oh. Ten o'clock. Group presentation. Urban drainage. Slide four is yours.
YOU: What's on slide four?
MIRA (phone): Drainage.
YOU: I don't know anything about drainage.
MIRA (phone): Nobody does. That's why it's a course. Also, I'm going to your work. What do you do all day?
YOU: Emails. Mostly emails about other emails.
MIRA (phone): Oh no.
> the course building. Ama waves.
N: Her course mates are lovely. They call you Mira, hand you a coffee and ask you about drains.
AMA: You ready, Mira?
YOU: So ready. Born ready. Drains.
> lecture hall, slide 4: a diagram of a pipe.
N: Slide four is a pipe. You talk about the pipe for three minutes. You say "flow" eleven times.
PROF: Unusual. Passionate. Mostly wrong. B minus.
AMA: That's her best mark yet.
N: You decide not to tell her.
> afternoon: Mira on the phone from your office, a stack of reports.
MIRA (phone): Your boss asked me how my weekend was, and then he listened to the answer. Is he okay?
YOU: That's new. He's been listening lately.
MIRA (phone): Also, I did all your emails. It took forty minutes.
YOU: That's three weeks of emails.
MIRA (phone): You're welcome.

## ACT 2
> afternoon, Mira's flat. Her phone lights up: her mum, her flatmates, a group called "Course Panic".
N: Her phone never stops. Her mum. Her flatmates. A group called "Course Panic". People ask her things all day, and she answers every one of them.
N: You get a strange feeling. You know her better than anyone. And you have never seen how tired she is.
> her flatmates knock: pizza night. They're kind. They are not her people yet.
N: Her flatmates knock for pizza night. They're kind, and they're funny, and they don't know that she hates olives. You pick the olives off without thinking.
AMA: How did you know she hates olives?
YOU: Lucky guess.
> evening. A call from Mira, quiet.
MIRA (phone): Hey. Can I ask you something?
YOU: Sure.
MIRA (phone): I needed your phone to check the train times. And I found your drafts.
~ 0.8
N: Your drafts. Forty messages you started, to her, over two years, and never sent.
MIRA (phone): "I'm proud of you." "I miss you being two minutes away." "Your new flat is small and I love it."
MIRA (phone): Why didn't you send any of these?
~ 0.6
YOU: I didn't want to bother you. You've got a whole new life.
MIRA (phone): You're in my whole new life. You're the person I tell things to.

## ACT 3
> 23:50, both phones, two flats.
N: Ten minutes to midnight.
MIRA (phone): Send them.
YOU: All of them?
MIRA (phone): All forty. Before we swap back. I want to get them on my own phone.
> you hit send, again and again. Her phone buzzes, buzzes, buzzes in your hand.
N: You hit send forty times. Her phone buzzes forty times in your hand, which is a very strange way to receive a hug.
MIRA (phone): Number twelve is just the word "sandwich".
YOU: That was a whole day. You'd know if you'd been there.
MIRA (phone): I'm sending you one back for every single one.
> 00:00. Swap back: you in your own bed, Theo snoring in the next room.
N: At midnight, you wake up in your own bed. Theo's snoring. There's a pan in the sink.
> your phone: a reply.
MIRA (text): Read them all. Sending you forty back. Pace yourself.
> buzz, buzz, buzz.
N: Your phone buzzes forty times.
~ 0.5
N: You read every one.

## END
~ 2.3
N (soft): What would your best friend find in your drafts?
