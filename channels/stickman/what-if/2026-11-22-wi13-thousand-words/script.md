# WI13 script: "What If You Only Had 1,000 Words a Day?" (v1 full, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person, present tense. Built on P1 (the channel's #1 Short). Stake: Theo, your little brother.
Rule (stated by 0:20, never changed): everyone gets 1,000 words a day, spoken or typed. A white counter floats over every head. At zero you're silent until midnight. Nods, hugs and pictures are free.

## HOOK
> your flat, night: Theo on the sofa, crying. Your counter: 14.
N: Your little brother is crying on the sofa.
N: And you have fourteen words left.
~ 0.5
N: In this world, everyone gets a thousand words a day. Spoken or typed.
N: Use them up, and you're silent until midnight.
~ 0.4
N: This morning, you had a thousand.

## ACT 1
> kitchen, bright morning
YOU: Morning!
N: Nine hundred and ninety-nine.
> phone: the group chat
N: Then the group chat starts a war about pineapple on pizza.
N: Six hundred words later, you win.
~ 0.3
N: At work, your boss asks for a quick update.
BOSS: Quick. Just the headlines.
N: It is not quick.
~ 0.3
> cafe, noon
N: At the café, the barista asks how your weekend was.
N: You tell him. All of it.
BARISTA: Cool. Oat milk?
~ 0.3
N: By lunch, you have forty-one.
N: By dinner, fourteen.
~ 0.5
N: And then Theo knocks on your door.
THEO: Can I tell you something? Something real?

## ACT 2
> your room, night
N: Theo has nine hundred and ninety-three words. He saved them all day.
THEO: I quit the football team. I didn't tell Mum.
THEO: Everyone thinks I'm lazy. I'm not. I just hate being bad at it.
THEO: Coach said I was the slowest one there. Everyone laughed.
THEO: So I stopped going. Three weeks ago.
N: Three weeks. You didn't notice.
N: You were busy spending words on people you'll never meet.
N: Every sentence you want to say is longer than fourteen words.
N: So you don't say any of them.
~ 0.4
N: You nod. Nods are free.
THEO: You think I'm a quitter.
N: You shake your head. Also free.
~ 0.5
N: Then your phone buzzes. It's Mum.
N: Is Theo okay? she writes. He's been so quiet.
N: Typing counts. One reply could cost you everything you have left.
N: You can answer her. Or you can answer him.
~ 0.6

## ACT 3
N: You turn the phone face down.
N: And you spend six words. Slowly.
YOU: You're not bad. You're just new.
N: Eight left.
THEO: That's it?
YOU: That's enough.
N: Six.
~ 0.3
N: You grab a pen. Pictures are free.
N: A tiny football. A tiny Theo. And a giant trophy.
THEO: That's not even a real trophy.
N: But he's smiling.
~ 0.4
N: He talks until his counter runs out. You listen until midnight.
N: At twelve, your counter clicks back to a thousand.
YOU: Okay. Now tell me everything.
~ 0.5
N: What would you say with fourteen words?
