# WI06 script: "What If You Could See How Many Chances You Have Left?" (v1, 2026-11-12, approved idea #4)
Narration: second person, present tense. Wry and observational in Act 1; tight and sparse in Act 2 (the narrator talks less as YOU talk less); warm in Act 3.
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · MIRA · BARISTA · BOSS · THEO (younger brother, NEW recurring) · MUM. `(phone)` call · `(text)` a sent message, read by the sender's voice.
`~ 0.8` = extra pause before the next line · `>` = stage direction (not spoken, adds a scene beat).
Continuity: Mira lives four hours away since WI04 (her course). Theo asks to stay at your place; he moves in for WI07.
Motif: a gold number badge over every head = how many more times that person will forgive you. Mum's badge is a wobbling ∞.

## HOOK
> cold open: café window at dusk, Mira's video-call photo on your phone with a red 1 over it
N: Everyone you know has a number now.
N: It's how many more times they'll forgive you.
~ 0.4
N: Your little brother. Three hundred and twelve.
N: Your mum.
~ 0.3
N: Doesn't even have a number.
~ 0.5
N: Your best friend has one.
~ 0.6
N: Just one.

## ACT 1
> café counter, morning. BARISTA: 40.
N: It takes you one coffee to work out the rule.
> you bump the counter; coffee splashes his apron
YOU: Oh no. Sorry, sorry—
BARISTA: Ah, don't worry about it.
N: Forty. Thirty-nine.
N: Every time someone forgives you, it goes down by one.
> park bench on the way to work. A toddler with 9,000; a stranger with 4.
N: A toddler in the park has nine thousand. Toddlers forgive everyone.
N: A man you have never met has four. You decide not to find out why.
> office. BOSS: 2.
N: Your boss has two. You have never been this early in your life.
BOSS: Morning. Is that a tie?
YOU: It felt like a tie day.
BOSS: It's Sunday.
> street, phone call. THEO on screen: 312.
THEO (phone): Can I borrow your charger? And your jacket. And forty quid.
YOU: You already have my charger.
THEO (phone): Then just the forty. Also, can I stay at yours for a bit?
N: Three hundred and twelve. Little brothers are built for this.
> mum on a video call, the ∞ wobbling
MUM (phone): You never call.
YOU: I'm calling now.
MUM (phone): Then you're forgiven. Did you eat?
N: The symbol over her head doesn't even flicker.
~ 0.6
N: And then there's Mira.

## ACT 2
> bedroom, night. Mira calling; the 1 glows red over her photo.
N: Four hours away, at her new course. With a one over her head.
N: One more mistake, and that's it. Whatever "it" is.
N: So you get careful.
N: You do the maths. If every mistake costs one chance, the only safe amount of Mira is none.
~ 0.4
N: Her call comes at nine. You let it ring. A call is twenty minutes of things you could say wrong.
MIRA (text): You alive?
N: You write a reply. Then a shorter one. Then a thumbs up.
> DAY 2. Photo of her new flat.
N: She sends a photo of her new flat. You don't say it's small. You don't say anything.
> DAY 3. Theo calls.
THEO (phone): Why are you being weird with Mira?
YOU: I'm not being weird.
THEO (phone): She texted me. Me. To ask if you're okay.
N: Mira never texts Theo. Theo can't even spell okay.
MIRA (text): Showcase on Friday. My first one. Will you come?
N: Four hours on a train. A whole evening of chances to get it wrong.
YOU (text): Can't. Work. I'll watch the stream?
~ 0.5
N: You send it.
~ 0.6
> the 1 over her photo clinks to 0
N: Somewhere four hours away, she forgives you.
~ 0.5
N: Zero.

## ACT 3
N: You spent her last chance by doing nothing at all.
> bedroom, lying awake, the phone glowing on the ceiling
N: That night, you work out what you were protecting. Not her. The number.
~ 0.5
N: And a zero has nothing left to protect.
> Friday, the train
N: So on Friday, you get on the train.
N: Four hours. For once, you don't rehearse a single sentence.
> showcase hall. You arrive late, stand at the back. Mira on a small stage beside her project.
N: You're late. You stand at the back. You clap too loud, at the wrong moment.
MIRA: That's my friend. He's weird.
> the audience laughs; Mira grins and carries on
N: The audience laughs. She keeps going. She's good at this. You knew she would be.
~ 0.6
> outside the hall, after
MIRA: You said you couldn't come.
YOU: I lied. I was scared of getting it wrong.
MIRA: So you just disappeared? That's the only thing that actually hurts.
~ 0.6
YOU: I know. I'm sorry.
~ 0.8
N: You wait for the number to go below zero.
~ 0.6
MIRA: You're here now. Buy me chips, and we're even.
~ 0.5
> the 0 over her head flickers… and becomes 1
N: Zero. Then, one.
N: It turns out the number doesn't only go down.
> walking, chips, steam, the city at night
N: You buy the chips. You tell her the flat isn't small. You tell her it's very small, and very her.
MIRA: Okay, now you're being weird again.
YOU: You said I was weird. On a stage. In front of everyone.
MIRA: I said you were my friend first.
~ 0.5
N: Over her head, the one glows, small and gold. You stop looking at it.

## END
~ 2.3
N (soft): Would you want to know how many chances you have left?
On screen: "Would YOU want to know?" + comment bubble + subscribe pill.
