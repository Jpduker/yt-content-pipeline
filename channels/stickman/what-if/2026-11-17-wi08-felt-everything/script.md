# WI08 script: "What If You Felt Everything Your Best Friend Felt?" (v1, 2026-11-12, approved idea #6)
Narration: second person, present tense, immersive and sensory: describe the body first (heart, hands, chest), the reason second.
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · MIRA · BOSS · COLL (Dana) · THEO. `(phone)` call · `(text)` a sent message. `~ 0.8` pause · `>` stage direction.
Continuity: Mira's first exam on her course (results the next morning). Theo still lives with you (WI07). Callback to WI03 (not answering what she hasn't said).
Motif: a soft ring of Mira's yellow light around YOUR chest; a small label beside it names her feeling (NERVOUS, HAPPY, TERRIFIED, SAD, PROUD). Its colour and pulse follow the feeling.

## HOOK
> cold open: dark bedroom, 6 a.m.; your chest ring pulsing fast, label NERVOUS
N: You wake up nervous. Your heart's racing. Your hands are wet.
N: Which is strange, because you've got nothing to be nervous about.
~ 0.4
N: Four hours away, your best friend has an exam at nine.
~ 0.6
N: From today, you feel everything she feels.

## ACT 1
N: Everything. The good, the bad, and the fourth coffee.
> kitchen, 8:55. Theo eating cereal.
THEO: Why are you breathing like that?
YOU: Exam.
THEO: You don't have an exam.
YOU: I know!
> office, 9:00. You are presenting. Label BLANK.
N: At nine, her exam starts, and your mind goes completely blank.
BOSS: So the numbers for March are—
YOU: I'm sorry. Who are you?
> office meeting, 10:00. You jitter, click a pen, tap a foot.
N: At ten, she has her fourth coffee. You have a meeting.
BOSS: Are you vibrating?
YOU: Big fan of this quarter.
> canteen, 12:00. Label HAPPY.
N: At twelve, her exam is over and she's happy. So you're happy. You're so happy, you nearly hug Dana.
COLL: Please don't.
N: At one, she eats a pastry. You can't taste it. But you have never loved a sandwich this much.
> office, 15:00. Label TERRIFIED.
N: At three, she watches a horror film with her new flatmates.
~ 0.4
N: You don't.
> you scream at your screen; the whole office turns
YOU: Sorry! Spreadsheet. Very scary numbers.
> bus stop, 17:30. Label ANNOYED. A pigeon.
N: At half five, her bus is late. You glare at a pigeon. The pigeon didn't do anything.
YOU: Sorry.
N: You just apologised to a pigeon. It's been a long day, and none of it was yours.

## ACT 2
> the flat, evening. Theo on the sofa. Label WORRIED.
N: By the evening, results are out tomorrow, and she's worried. So you're worried. And you can't turn it off.
THEO: Why are you pacing?
YOU: I'm not pacing. I'm walking with purpose.
THEO: Can you feel me as well?
YOU: No.
THEO: Good.
> texting
YOU (text): Did you sleep? Did you eat? Did you check question six?
MIRA (text): Why are you being my mum?
N: Because her worry is sitting in your chest, and you want it out.
N: You tell yourself you're helping. You're not. You just want the feeling to stop.
YOU (text): Just relax. It'll be fine.
~ 0.4
N: Her worry goes up. You can feel exactly how much.
MIRA (text): Wow. Thanks. Going to bed.
> label: ANNOYED, then SAD
N: Now she's annoyed. And then, quietly, something else.
N: Something heavy. Like a wet coat you can't take off.

## ACT 3
> midnight, your bedroom, dark; the ring dim and blue-grey
N: At midnight, you're lying in the dark, sad for a reason that isn't yours.
N: So you call her.
MIRA (phone): I'm fine.
~ 0.5
N: She's not. You can feel she's not.
N: You want to fix it. Tell her the exam went well. Tell her the course is worth it. Tell her she'll make friends.
~ 0.5
N: But once, at a café, you learned not to answer things she hadn't said.
~ 0.6
YOU: You don't have to be fine.
~ 0.9
MIRA (phone): I just miss home. That's all. It's stupid.
YOU: It's not stupid.
N: And then you don't say anything else. You just stay on the line.
> split screen: two dark rooms, two phone lights
N: Five minutes. Ten. Neither of you talks.
N: And the heavy coat gets lighter. Not gone. Shared.
N: You've felt her nervous, happy, terrified and annoyed today. This is the first feeling you didn't try to make go away.
~ 0.7
MIRA (phone): Are you still there?
YOU: I'm here.
MIRA (phone): You're smiling. I can hear it.
MIRA (phone): Will you stay on until I fall asleep?
YOU: Yeah.
~ 0.6
N: She falls asleep at twenty to one. You can feel it, like a lamp being turned down slowly in another city.
> morning, kitchen, 9:00. Toast. Theo.
N: The next morning, at nine, a feeling hits you so hard you drop your toast.
> the ring blazes gold; label PROUD
YOU: She passed!
THEO: Who passed what?
YOU: Everything!
THEO: Did you drop your toast?
YOU: Worth it.
> phone buzzes
MIRA (text): Passed! Did you feel that?
YOU (text): Felt it. Dropped my toast.

## END
~ 2.3
N (soft): Would you want to feel everything they feel?
On screen: "Would YOU want to feel it?" + comment bubble + subscribe pill.
