## WI03 · What If You Could See Every Text People Almost Sent You?
Hook (≈ 14 s):
N: You wake up, and your phone is full of messages nobody sent.
N: Your boss typed, "You're fired." Then deleted it.
N: Your mum typed, "Are you eating?" Eleven times.
N: And at three a.m., your best friend typed three words… and held backspace.
MIRA-T (ghost bubble): I'm not okay.
N: She never sent it. You could answer anyway.
Beats: ① comedy: the group chat's deleted hoodie jokes, the pizza place's deleted apology; counter "UNSENT 1 → 212". ② useful: you fix the report before
the boss asks, cover for the colleague who didn't dare ask. ③ cost: you answer things nobody said ("How did you know?"), people pull back. ④ heart: you
call mum before she types it a twelfth time. ⑤ Mira at coffee: "I'm fine." Choice: reply to the unsent text, or give her the room to send it.
Ending: you type "I saw—", delete it (your own ghost bubble appears), send "Coffee? No reason." She tells you herself. Question: "Would YOU read them?"
New side characters: BOSS (blue tie, returns), MUM (new, red cardigan). Sets: bedroom, office, café (all exist) + phone-screen inserts.

## WI04 · What If You Could See Who's About to Leave Your Life?
Hook (≈ 13 s):
N: One morning, everyone you know has a number over their head.
N: Your barista: two thousand days. Your boss: four hundred and twelve.
N: Your best friend…
N: Three.
N: It doesn't say why. It only says how long.
Beats: ① what the number means: the neighbour at 0 drives off in a moving van. ② comedy: the colleague at 12 (you find the resignation letter), the gym
you never go to at 0. ③ you try to raise Mira's number: gifts, plans, perfect behaviour; it still ticks 3 → 2. ④ you imagine the worst (a fight, an illness).
⑤ truth: she got into the course in another city and didn't know how to tell you. When she hesitates, her number flickers 2 → 900. Choice: ask her to stay,
or help her pack. Ending: you carry the box; 0 on the platform; your phone buzzes: "Home safe. Call you tonight?" The number counted distance, not the friendship.
Question: "Would you ask them to stay?" New side characters: NEIGHBOUR (orange scarf), COLL (purple bun, returns). New set: train platform (train set exists).

## WI05 · What If You Could See Who Cried Because of You?
Hook (≈ 13 s):
N: Today there's a small blue teardrop over some people's heads.
N: It takes you until lunch to work out what it means.
N: They've cried. Because of you.
N: The bus driver has one. Your mum has forty-one.
N: Your best friend got hers… last night.
Beats: ① comedy: who are these people? (the kid whose kite you cut, the referee, your old landlord). ② the apology tour, counter going down. ③ mum, 41:
you call to say sorry; she laughs: "Half of those were your school plays." Not every tear was pain. ④ so which kind is Mira's? You rehearse an apology
for something you can't name. ⑤ Choice: ask her, or keep guessing. Ending: she cried reading the card you forgot you wrote; in the café window your own
reflection has a teardrop too, with her colour on it. Question: "Would you want to know?"
New side characters: MUM (returns from WI03), BUS DRIVER (teal cap). Sets: street/bus (train set re-dressed), café, bedroom.
