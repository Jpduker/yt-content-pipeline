# WI07 script: "What If Every Apology Erased What You Did?" (v1, 2026-11-12, approved idea #5)
Narration: second person, present tense, confessional: guilt told quietly, like admitting something late at night.
Tags: N narrator · YOU hero · THEO (brother, now staying with you) · BOSS · BARISTA · MIRA. `(phone)` call. `~ 0.8` pause · `>` stage direction.
Continuity: Theo moved in after WI06 ("can I stay at yours for a bit?"). Mira's birthday falls this week (she lives four hours away).
Motif: when YOU say "sorry", a white eraser shimmer wipes a small memory picture above the listener's head. A faint grey smudge stays: the feeling without the memory.

## HOOK
> cold open: a broken game controller on the floor; Theo frozen mid-shout
N: Today, every time you say sorry, the other person forgets what you did.
~ 0.3
N: Not forgives. Forgets.
N: You break your brother's controller.
YOU: Sorry, Theo.
> eraser shimmer over Theo's head
THEO: Sorry for what?
~ 0.6
N: It's the best day of your life.
~ 0.6
N: For about a week.

## ACT 1
> the flat, Monday. Four bags, a console, Theo on the sofa.
N: Theo moved in on Monday. Temporarily. With four bags, a games console, and no plan.
> office, 9:20
N: On Tuesday, you're twenty minutes late.
BOSS: Twenty minutes. Again. Do you know what time—
YOU: Sorry.
> shimmer
BOSS: Ah, good morning! Early, as always.
> café counter
N: You forget your wallet at the café. Third time this month.
YOU: Sorry. I'll pay you tomorrow.
BARISTA: Pay me for what?
N: Free coffee. You decide not to think about it too hard.
> street: you reverse your bike into a parked scooter
N: You knock over a stranger's scooter.
YOU: Oh no. Sorry!
> shimmer
STRANGER: What scooter?
N: By Tuesday night, you've said sorry eleven times, and you've been in trouble zero.
~ 0.4
N: You don't notice the little grey smudge it leaves over everyone's head.
N: Not yet.

## ACT 2
> the flat, Wednesday, 2 a.m. Theo gaming, loud.
N: On Wednesday, Theo plays until two in the morning.
YOU: Theo. It's two a.m.
THEO: Five more minutes.
N: And you say the thing you've been thinking since Monday.
YOU: You know why nobody lets you stay anywhere? Because you're exhausting.
~ 0.8
> Theo's face drops; he looks at the floor
~ 0.6
YOU: Sorry.
> shimmer. The memory wipes. A grey smudge stays.
THEO: Sorry for what?
~ 0.5
N: He doesn't remember. But he doesn't pick the controller back up either.
> kitchen, Thursday morning. Theo quiet, washing your mug.
N: The next morning, he's quiet. He washes your mug. He asks if he's in the way.
THEO: Did I do something? You can tell me.
YOU: No. You didn't do anything.
N: That's the problem. He didn't. You did. And he's the one carrying it.
> walk to work: grey smudges over the boss, the barista, the man from the lift
N: Now you see the smudges everywhere. Over your boss. Over the barista. Small grey clouds where a memory used to be.
> café counter; the barista rubs his forehead
BARISTA: Weird week. I keep feeling annoyed at someone, and I can't remember who.
YOU: That's weird.
BARISTA: You want your usual? It's on— Actually. Have you paid me lately?
N: The memory goes. The feeling stays. And nobody knows where it came from.

## ACT 3
> Friday, 10 p.m. Your phone: Mira.
N: Then, on Friday, you forget Mira's birthday.
MIRA (phone): Hey. So. It's ten p.m.
~ 0.6
N: The word is right there. Sorry. One word, and she'll never know.
~ 0.5
N: And she'll still go to bed sad, and not know why.
~ 0.9
YOU: Mira. I forgot your birthday. Completely. And I'm not going to say the word, because I want you to stay mad at me.
MIRA (phone): That is the weirdest apology I have ever heard.
YOU: It's not an apology. It's a confession.
MIRA (phone): Fine. I'm mad. You owe me a cake. A big one.
~ 0.6
MIRA (phone): Okay. I'm a bit less mad.
YOU: Saturday. Cake. In person.
MIRA (phone): It's four hours on a train.
YOU: It's going to be a very big cake.
N: No shimmer. No smudge. Just Mira, remembering, and forgiving you anyway.
N: It's the first time all week anyone has.
> the flat. Theo on the sofa, controller untouched.
N: Then you go and sit next to Theo.
YOU: On Wednesday, I said something horrible to you. You don't remember it. I do.
THEO: What did you say?
N: You tell him. Word for word.
~ 0.7
THEO: Wow. That's rude.
YOU: Yeah.
THEO: I am a bit exhausting, though.
YOU: You're a lot exhausting. Stay anyway.
~ 0.6
> the smudge over his head thins out, like breath on a window
N: The grey smudge over his head fades, like breath on a window.
THEO: Can I have your controller, then?
YOU: No.
> Saturday: the train, a huge cake box on your lap; then a bench with Mira, cake everywhere
N: On Saturday, you carry a very large cake for four hours.
N: Mira eats a third of it on a bench, and tells you she's still a little bit mad.
N: You let her be.

## END
~ 2.3
N (soft): Would you rather be forgiven, or forgotten?
