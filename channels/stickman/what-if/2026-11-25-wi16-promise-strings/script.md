# WI16 script: "What If Every Promise You Made Was a String?" (v1, 2026-11-17)
Narration: second person. Built on O4 (#6 Short). Stakes: Mum and Mira.
Rule (by 0:20): every promise ties a glowing string from your wrist to the other person. Keep it and it turns gold and fades. Break it and it snaps. A string can only be cut by the person it's tied to.

## HOOK
> street, morning: your wrist trailing dozens of glowing strings into the city
N: Your wrist has forty-one strings tied to it this morning.
N: And one of them just snapped.
~ 0.4
N: In this world, every promise you make becomes a string.
N: Keep it, and it turns gold and fades away.
N: Break it, and it snaps. Everybody hears it.

## ACT 1
> office
N: You promise your boss the report by Friday.
N: You promise Theo you'll fix his bike.
N: Mira texts. She's moving flats on Sunday. You promise to help.
N: And Mum. You promised Mum you'd visit on Sunday. Three weeks ago.
N: That one's the thickest. It hums when you walk past her photo.

## ACT 2
> week montage
N: Friday, the report string snaps. Your boss doesn't even look up.
N: Saturday, Theo's bike string snaps. He looks up for a long time.
~ 0.3
N: Then Sunday comes.
N: Two strings. Pulling in opposite directions.
N: Mum's house is east. Mira's flat is west.
N: Whichever way you walk, one of them breaks.
~ 0.6

## ACT 3
N: You call Mira.
MIRA (phone): Let me guess. Your mum.
YOU: I'm sorry. I promised her first.
MIRA (phone): Then go.
N: And Mira does something you didn't know was possible.
N: She cuts her own string. Gently. So it doesn't snap.
MIRA (phone): There. Now you didn't break anything.
~ 0.4
N: You spend Sunday with Mum. Her string turns gold.
N: On Monday, you knock on Mira's door with pizza.
YOU: Next Saturday. Boxes. Promise.
N: She ties the new string herself.
N: Who holds the thickest string to you?
