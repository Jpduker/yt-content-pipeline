# Stickman · "Floor Minus" (draft for Jp): a Day-counter survival serial with a twist
**Logline:** You step into an elevator that only goes DOWN. Every day the doors open on a new floor, each one a strange little world
with one rule. You have to survive the rule until the doors open again. Nobody knows what's at the bottom.
**Why:** NexLev shows tiny stickman channels breaking out with "Day 1 → Day 30/100, trapped in X" survival (up to 47× their average);
zombies are saturated, the format isn't. Ours adds a mystery ladder (what's at the bottom?) and a new world every day for variety.
**Cast (consistent):** You (round head, tuft, blue trousers: our stick-you) · Nora (another passenger, practical, red scarf) ·
the Operator (a voice from a speaker grille, polite and wrong) · counters on screen: DAY, FLOOR, FOOD, BATTERY.
**Narrator:** second person ("you"), Kokoro am_fenrir; Nora gets her own voice (af_bella or similar).
**Captions:** karaoke chunks (Jp liked them on WI01).

## Episode 1 · "Day 1 to Day 3" (≈5 min, 16:9) · beat sheet
0:00 Hook: "You have 3 floors to learn the rules. Most people don't make it past one." Elevator dings. -1.
0:20 **Floor -1, The Polite Floor:** everyone must say "thank you" before every action; break the rule and the floor tilts. Comedy +
     danger. You learn to survive; you meet Nora. Day 1 ends, the doors close, FOOD 3→2.
1:50 **Floor -2, The Floor Where It Rains Upward:** water falls toward the ceiling; you climb furniture to breathe; resource scramble.
     You find a note in your OWN handwriting: "Don't trust the Operator." Day 2.
3:30 **Floor -3, The Quiet Floor:** any sound above a whisper makes the lights go out one by one. The Operator starts "helping".
     Nora disappears for 10 seconds; when she comes back her scarf is blue.
4:40 **Cliffhanger:** doors open on -4. The floor is a perfect copy of your apartment. Someone is already sitting on your sofa. It's you.
     "Day 4: next Saturday."
**Shorts:** each floor's rule as a 40 s Short ("What if every floor had one rule?"), ending on that floor's twist.
**Look upgrade (vs WI01):** 16:9 sets with depth and lighting per floor (warm, wet-blue, near-dark), real physics gags (tilt, upward rain),
on-screen counters as diegetic elevator displays, camera that follows, our karaoke captions.
