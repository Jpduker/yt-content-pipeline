# Channel plan (v1, 2026-11-05)
Built from NexLev research (memory/CHANNELS.md), the yt-plan method (one anchor + Shorts cut from it, not daily posting),
and the channel studies below. Numbers are NexLev estimates.

## What the evidence says
- Puzzle/riddle Shorts have the biggest outliers (Rezon median 6.1M/Short, Big Brain Chess 845K) but pay ≈$0.10 per 1,000 views.
- Long-form story / explainer videos pay ≈$2–4 per 1,000 and small channels break out with them:
  - Scenario Based (joined 2026-10-05, 34 videos, 1.02M views): long-form 9–37 min "1 Year in…" survival stories; its best are NUMBERED SEQUELS (Fog Apocalypse → part 4). Its Shorts barely work (≤3.6K).
  - Paint It Simple (15 videos, 3.2M views): 9-min stickman history explainers; one hit = 2.5M.
- So: long-form serial episodes earn and build binge-watching; Shorts must be strong on their own (riddles, cliffhangers), not trailers.

## Channel 1: the island serial (riddles + story, one world)
- World: Gus the lighthouse keeper's island. Cast grows each episode (EP02 adds Pip the seagull, Mabel the cat, Rollo the mouse).
- Style: mid-century cartoon (proven in EP01, Jp loved it).
- **Long-form chapters (anchor, weekly, 8–12 min):** one season-long mystery; each chapter the cast must solve 5–7 riddles to move forward; pause-and-solve countdowns; ends on a cliffhanger. Season 1 working title: "The Keeper's Island".
- **Riddle Shorts (3/week):** each one is a riddle FROM that week's chapter, 30–45 s, loops; caption/description points to the chapter.
- **"Old Tales of the Island" (1/week Short, later also long):** Gus tells a dark legend of the island in a storybook style (Ukiyo-e first; shadow-puppet, papercut, crayon-book later). Each legend secretly foreshadows the season mystery, so the fairy tales are part of the serial, not a different channel.
- Riddles: original only from EP02 on. Keep a riddle bank in library/hooks/riddles.md.

## Channel 2 (test): stickman scenarios
- Different audience and look, so a separate channel, tested before launch.
- Style: Paint It Simple's 2D look (big round white heads, expressive faces, ink limbs, illustrated colour backgrounds, hand-drawn arrows) + Scenario Based's real motion (rigged walks, gestures, camera moves, weather) + our kinetic captions.
- Format: second-person "What if you…" comedy/scenario stories; Shorts first to find the voice, then serialized long-form ("1 Day / 1 Year as…") if a Short works.

## Weekly cadence (channel 1, once launched)
| Day | Format | What |
|---|---|---|
| Sat | Long-form chapter | The anchor |
| Mon / Wed / Fri | Riddle Short | Cut from the chapter |
| Thu | Old Tale Short | Legend that foreshadows next chapter |
Jp's time: ~2–3 h/week (review, upload, reply to comments). Claude builds everything else. If a week goes wrong, drop the Old Tale first, never the chapter.

## Risks to manage
- **Made for kids:** cartoon riddles with animals can be classified "made for kids" — then comments are off (kills "comment your answer") and ads are limited. Write for a general/family-plus-adult audience (mystery tone, clever humour) and set the audience setting honestly. (Verify current YouTube rules before launch.)
- **Inauthentic-content policy:** original serial story, original riddles, own characters, own music = the safest position. No templated reskins.
- Same voice for Gus across everything (Kokoro am_michael).
