# Jp's taste
Evidence-based. Update after every reaction Jp gives (see workflows/retro.md). Last updated: 2026-11-05.

## Loves
- **A world, not screenshots.** The explanation happens inside a built place: toy-theatre diorama (ckojr), voxel night city (a00e), cosy studio set (gf92), neon 80s city (City Lights demo).
- **A mascot with expressions** whose reactions carry the comedy (gf92 cube buddy, a00e red cube + voxel hero, ckojr block mascots, one colour per role).
- **A story arc even in an explainer**: problem, twist, payoff. a00e: "3:12 AM, 6 views" → "2.6M views" → "not AI video, it's code".
- **Kinetic captions**: one word at a time, keyword highlighted in yellow (a00e), cut-paper ransom-note words (ckojr).
- **Props that count and fill**: counters 0→352, checklists ticking, calendars filling, "STEP 1/4" progress bars.
- **Cinematic light and palette**: warm orange/brown cosy (gf92, ckojr), deep blue night with neon (a00e, City Lights).
- **Anime look**: rewatched the 80s cel-anime render twice; the anime interview reel (lhuh) was the first inspiration.
- **Fast rhythm**: a new shot about every 3.7 s in the favourites.
- **Riddles done well**: clean flat cutaway world, a camera that follows, a countdown, a reveal, and a seamless loop back to frame 1 (oig0).
- **CTA inside the world**: "Comment STYLE", a fake comment box typing "YOUTUBE".

## Dislikes
- Flat generic clip-art character over UI screenshots, long static holds (~15 s per shot), dry tutorial pacing (x7q7).
- Talking-head presenter cut with doc screenshots (r4wg). On-camera faces in general: our channels are **faceless**.
- Static placeholder visuals with no world (the v1 ffmpeg proof).
