## NexLev research (2026-11-04)
All numbers are NexLev estimates (outlier = video or channel views vs peers/average; revenue = AdSense estimate only). Outliers show what is possible, not what is typical.

**Money reality:** Shorts pay very little: Rezon ≈750M views → ≈$75K total (≈$0.10 per 1,000 views). Long-form story channels earn ≈$2–4 RPM. Shorts build reach and subscribers; long-form pays.

### Riddles and puzzles: strongest signal
- Rezon (288K subs, outlier 8.8, median 6.1M views/Short): interactive "wait for the end" puzzles.
- "funny Shorts" (47K, outlier 5.0, median 398K): visual puzzles: "Can you separate the old man and woman?" (40M), "How to divide 10 liters into 2 equal parts?" (28M).
- Big Brain Chess (58K, outlier 4.0, median 845K): code-renderable 3D chess puzzles ("X Shaped Chess Puzzle" 24M).
- Long-form compilations on tiny channels: "15 Riddles Only Geniuses Can Solve" (740K views, 7.9×), "15 Tricky Riddles…" (560K, 22×), "15 Impossible Visual Riddles" (180K on a 1.4K-sub channel, 69×). ≈10 min, monetizable.
- Pattern: one clear question, a visual the viewer can try to solve, countdown, reveal, "comment your answer".

### Animated story Shorts: strong
- Brain Craftt (189K, outlier 3.4, median 1.5M): dark fairy-tale hooks: "The King Who Turned People Into Statues" (25M), "He Ate an Entire Country… Then Got Betrayed" (10M).
- Long-form folktale channels are crowded with AI-image channels; RPM ≈$2–4. Outliers exist (African folktale 1.06M views on a 3.8K-sub channel, 24.6×).
- Our edge: distinctive code-rendered styles (shadow-puppet, papercut-red, ukiyoe, crayon-book) vs generic AI images.

### Stickman / cartoon comedy Shorts: strong, volume game
- Little matchstick man (88K, outlier 6.4, median 1.35M, 866 uploads): workplace humour.
- Flatline Funnies (39K, 3.3): absurd "what if" science ("If you drank an entire water tower" 21M).
- CoupleStickfigure (73K, 2.9): relationship/office jokes. One joke per Short, very high upload counts.

### Motivation: original animated short films win
- "DISCIPLINE | An Animated Short Film" (459K on a 5.5K-sub channel, 32.8×, 2.6 min, not AI).
- Animated psychology/discipline explainers: PsychToons "Psychology of Discipline" (2.3M, 19.6×), Jead "How To Force Your Brain To Do Hard Things" (1.6M, 30.7×).

### Tech explainer with mascot: weak evidence
- Few clean matches. AI Code Clash ("ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini — Fractal Tree Animation", 14M) shows code-made visuals can go viral, but revenue is tiny. Keep as a later idea.

### Recommendation (Claude, 2026-11-04)
1. Channel #1: **riddles and visual puzzles**: Shorts for reach + 10-min compilations for revenue. Easiest to code-render, Jp likes the format, strongest outliers.
2. Channel #2: **dark fairy-tale / fable Shorts** in lemo styles: distinctive art, strong Brain Craftt signal.
3. Later: stickman comedy (writing-heavy), motivation short films (one great film > many posts).
